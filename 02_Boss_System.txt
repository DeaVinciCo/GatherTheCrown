=== BOSS SYSTEM ===

TIER 1 — Mini-Bosses (Trial Beasts)
- Location: Mini-Games & early Side Quests
- Duration: 60–90 seconds
- Style: Reflex/puzzle-based
- Drops: Rare fruit, boosts, blueprint fragments

TIER 2 — Roaming Bosses (Worldbinders)
- Location: Side Quests / PvE Dungeons
- Duration: 3–5 minutes
- Mechanics: 2–3 elemental phases, minor enrages
- Drops: Unique armor/creat skin mods, advanced food, lore entries

TIER 3 — Crown Sentinels
- Location: Crown Trials (PvE) / Crownfall Gauntlet Events
- Duration: 4–7 minutes
- Mechanics: Stagger phases, synced attacks, reactive to creat+char synergy
- Drops: Mythic Crown Shards, permanent stat boosts, unique titles

TIER 4 — Story Mode Bosses (Reignlords)
- Location: Story Mode (Path of the Crown)
- Duration: 5 min → 10 min → 15 min (progressive levels)
- Mechanics: Multiple enrages, forcefield resistance, Sync Mode co-op attacks, environmental shifts, Crown Combo phase (30+ hits = weaken core)
- Drops: Crown Fragments (core progression), Legendary Creat food, Region unlock keys, Unique transformations

TIER 5 — Faction Raids
- Location: Faction Battles
- Style: Massive, 10-player synced fights
- Scaling: Based on average player crown tier
- Rewards: Custom faction gear, banners, keys, Keepsake entry tokens

=== BOSS EVOLUTION SYSTEM ===
- Bosses evolve each story arc, adapting elementally to player's creat/character build
- Critical debuffs required (fire crit vs ice shield to stagger, lightning crit vs earth boss to stun)
- Each defeat drops loot for next stage progression
- Boss forms change based on player element (e.g., Fire Queen → Frostflame → Emberstorm)

BOSS WEAKNESS TRIGGER SYSTEM:
Fire Boss → Ice Crit = Freeze (4 sec, slows attacks)
Ice Boss → Fire Crit = Burn (damage over time)
Lightning Boss → Earth Crit = Blind (lowers accuracy)
Earth Boss → Lightning Crit = Stun (3 sec, open for combo)
Shadow Boss → Light Crit = Silence spells (5 sec)
Light Boss → Shadow Crit = Confuse (random attack pattern)
