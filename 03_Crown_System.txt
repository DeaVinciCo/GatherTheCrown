=== CROWN SYSTEM ===

CROWN PUZZLE MECHANICS:
- Crowns forged from shard pieces (metals) + gems (elements) in puzzle system
- Crown puzzles: ghost-outline UI where shards and gems slot into specific shapes
- Collect shards/gems to build multiple crowns simultaneously
- Crowns give temporary stat boosts when equipped (attack, crit, defense, stealth, speed)
- Durability: Crowns shatter after use (# battles or minutes)
- Shatter effect: pieces scatter, pulse 8 seconds, then vanish permanently (NO RECOVERY)
- Multiple crowns of same type can be crafted and stockpiled
- Only one crown active at a time
- Completing crowns grants: loot, titles, cosmetics, permanent buffs, blueprint unlocks, aura effects

=== CROWN TYPES BY MODE ===

Story Mode: Sovereign's Diadem
- Metals: Starforged, Aethersteel
- Gems: Painite, Alexandrite, Jeremejevite
- Rarity: Mythic (ultra rare), Legendary, Epic

Melee Mode: Duelist's Crest
- Metals: Hardened Gold, Silver, Bronze
- Gems: Spinel, Topaz, Sapphire
- Rarity: Epic, Rare, Uncommon

Racing Mode: Racer's Torque
- Metals: Lightweight Osmium, Aethersteel
- Gems: Quartz, Sapphire, Jeremejevite
- Rarity: Rare, Uncommon, Common

Mini-Games: Tactician's Band
- Metals: Silver, Bronze
- Gems: Quartz, Topaz
- Rarity: Common, Uncommon

PvE Dungeon Mode: Warden's Circlet
- Metals: Bixbite, Gold
- Gems: Alexandrite, Spinel, Painite
- Rarity: Legendary, Epic, Rare

PvP Competitive: Conqueror's Halo
- Metals: Osmium, Starforged, Bixbite
- Gems: Painite, Jeremejevite, Alexandrite
- Rarity: Mythic, Legendary

Side Quests: Seeker's Laurel
- Metals: Silver, Bronze + Aethersteel inlays
- Gems: Quartz, Spinel, Topaz
- Rarity: Rare, Uncommon

META CROWN: Crown of the Seven Realms
- Metals: Starforged
- Gems: Painite, Alexandrite, Jeremejevite
- Effect: Permanent +15% boost to ALL stats
- Unlocks: Mythfall Gate (special realm portal)
- Visibility: Only visible to other crown-complete players
