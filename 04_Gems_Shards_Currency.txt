STACKING BONUS:
- Holding 10+ of one gem type = Power Surge (2x ability buff in battle)
- Sets unlock burst abilities (Blue+Red = Steam AoE)
- Sell/trade values scale by rarity

=== CURRENCY SYSTEM ===

Gold Coins (GC): Main spendable currency
- Earned from: matches, races, loot, quests
- Gameplay earnings: 75M (avg), 200M (max)

Gems: Elemental boosts, trading, socketing crowns

Shards: Puzzle pieces for forging crowns

Bixbite: Mythic currency from Divine fights only
- Used for: ultimate crafting, legendary forging, special cosmetics

Soulshards: From fallen foes
- Used for: crafting spells, bonding creats

PLACEMENT MEDALS (RACES):
1st Place: Rhodium (rare, shiny, white metal)
2nd Place: Nickel (durable, practical)
3rd Place: Copper (classic, accessible)
