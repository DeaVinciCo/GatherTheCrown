=== WEAPONS & TOOLS SYSTEM ===

STARTER WEAPONS (basic kit before crafting):
- Wooden Sword (short blade, weak but balanced)
- Stone Dagger (fast, low damage, high crit rate)
- Wooden Bow + Flint Arrows (long range, low durability)
- Wooden Staff (basic magic focus, no elemental buffs)
- Training Spear (reach advantage, brittle)
- Wooden Shield (blocks 1–2 hits before breaking)
- Slingshot (pebbles, weak, stuns small foes)

BASIC UPGRADE PATH:
- Iron Sword / Iron Dagger
- Steel Axe / Hatchet (can also chop wood)
- Hunting Spear (iron tip, better reach, higher damage)
- Shortbow + Iron Arrowheads
- Wooden Crossbow (slower, higher damage than bow)
- Basic Mage Staff (socket for 1 gem: fire, ice, or lightning)
- Round Iron Shield
- Blacksmith Hammer (dual-use: crafting + blunt weapon)

TOOLS (non-combat but upgradable):
- Pickaxe (ore mining, doubles as blunt weapon)
- Hatchet (wood harvesting, doubles as axe weapon)
- Farming Sickle (crop harvest, doubles as slicing weapon)
- Fishing Rod (food source, can hook small items)
- Torch (light source, basic fire damage)
- Shovel (digging, heavy blunt weapon if needed)

ELEMENTAL / SPECIALIZED WEAPONS:
- Flameblade (sword with fire gem socket)
- Froststaff (staff with ice gem, shoots frost bolts)
- Stormbow (bow socketed with lightning gem, chain-shot arrows)
- Shadow Dagger (poison/bleed effect, stealth boost)
- Earthshaker Hammer (stone + shard crafted, quake AoE)
- Crystal Pike (crafted with gems, pierces armor)
- Lightbringer Sword (socketed with clear/light gem, blinds foes)
- Dual Blades (crafted upgrade from daggers, high combo speed)

CROWN-WEAPON TIER (LEGENDARY):
- Crownblade (crafted from crown shards + gems, emits aura)
- Crown Staff (amplifies spells based on equipped gem set)
- Crownbow (fires gem-charged arrows)
- Crownshield (absorbs damage, then explodes in elemental burst)
- Relic Weapons (boss-specific drops: Ashthrone's Ember Scythe, Hollow Monarch's Voidfang)

WEAPON PROGRESSION:
Starter Kit → 1 weapon + 1 tool chosen based on character class
Upgrades → Found/crafted from shards + metals (iron, bronze, silver, gold, osmium)
Elemental Weapons → Only unlocked once gems socketed into crafted base weapon
Crown Weapons → Require completed crowns + rare boss loot (endgame tier)
