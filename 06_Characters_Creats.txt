=== CHARACTER FEATURES & TRAITS ===

CHARACTER SYSTEM:
- 3 characters per account max
- Each bonded with 3 creats
- First-ever creat = permanent (cannot delete)
- Reset requires: Story Mode completion + 50M GC

BASE STATS:
- Strength, Agility, Stamina, Wisdom, Luck, Charisma
- Certain crowns & gems amplify specific stats

TRAITS BY FACTION & ELEMENT:
- Fire Rider: higher crit rate
- Water Rider: better regen
- Earth Rider: increased defense
- Storm Rider: speed boost
- Light Rider: healing amplification
- Shadow Rider: stealth enhancement

APPEARANCE OPTIONS:
- Skin Tones: pale, fair, olive, tan, bronze, deep brown, ebony, gray-toned (shadow folk), glowing (mystics)
- Hair: long, short, braided, shaved, curly, wavy, straight, ponytails, buns, mohawks, locs, shaved sides
- Hair Colors: black, brown, blonde, red, white, silver, blue, purple, green, gold, fiery ombre, shadow fade
- Eye Colors: brown, blue, green, hazel, gold, silver, red, violet, glowing (elemental)
- Face Features: scars, freckles, tattoos, warpaint, glowing runes, piercings
- Body Types: lean, muscular, stocky, tall, short, agile, armored
- Clothing Styles: medieval armor, robes, leather gear, faction-specific colors, cloaks, crowns

=== CREAT FEATURES & TRAITS ===

ELEMENTAL AFFINITIES:
Fire, Water, Earth, Shadow, Light, Storm, Void, Arcane

GROWTH SYSTEM:
- Fed with fruits, rare foods, or shards
- Can evolve, unlock new forms, learn combo attacks with rider
- Battle together raises bond meter → unlocks Sync Mode
- Higher bond = higher crit synergy during boss fights

CREAT ROLES:
- Tank (Earth, Shadow)
- DPS (Fire, Storm, Void)
- Support (Water, Light)

CREAT TRAITS:
- Loyalty (won't abandon rider)
- Temperament (aggressive, cautious, curious)
- Unique passive perks (Shadow creat cloaks rider when idle, Water creat heals over time)

APPEARANCE OPTIONS:
- Skin/Scale/Hide Colors: natural tones (gray, brown, black, green, red, blue), elemental variants (lava glow, frost shimmer, storm crackle, shadow fade, radiant)
- Body Types: drake, serpent, boar, wolf, bird, insectoid, golem
- Features: horns, tusks, feathers, glowing eyes, extra limbs, wings, crystalline growths, scars
- Size Variants: hatchling, adolescent, full-grown, elder
- Temperament: loyal, aggressive, cunning, cautious, curious

ELEMENTAL AURAS (always visible):
- FIRE: ember motes, orange rim light
- WATER: soft blue caustics, drip FX
- EARTH: dust flecks, cracked soil prints
- STORM: tiny spark forks, after-image on dash
- LIGHT: soft bloom at edges, white specks
- SHADOW: violet haze, subtle blur mask
- ICE: frost mist cone, sparkle flecks
