=== WORLD DESIGN & BIOMES ===

CORE ZONES:
- Castles & fortified towns (hub areas, crown halls, shops)
- Forests & swamps (roaming bosses, creat dens)
- Ruins & temples (story bosses, shard caches)
- Arenas (PvP combat zones)
- Circuits & tracks (Racing mode)
- Oceans & moats (aquatic roaming bosses)

=== FACTION ZONES ===

INFERNAL KIN (Volcano/Fire Caves):
- Environment: Volcanoes, ember caves
- Exclusive Materials: Emberium shards, Molten Pearl
- Palette: Black, red, orange, ember gold
- Ground: Charred tiles, ash drift, safe basalt plates, molten cracks (animated)
- Vegetation: Firecaps (mushrooms), ember fruit (rare)
- Architecture: Blackened fort metalwork, chain lifts, slag heaps, forges (sparks)

TIDEBORN (Ocean/Water Reefs):
- Environment: Ocean caves, reefs
- Exclusive Materials: Dusktide Opals, Tideglass
- Palette: Blues, teals, sandy golds
- Water: Saltwater wave loop, foam crest tiles, tide pools
- Shore: Wet/dry sand transitions, rock outcrops, driftwood, shells
- Vegetation: Sea grapes, coconuts, date palms, fish racks, kelp piles
- Architecture: Stilted shacks, rope piers, lighthouses (turning beam), harbor cranes

SKY NOMADS (Storm/Lightning Isles):
- Environment: Floating islands
- Exclusive Materials: Skycrystals, Lunasteel
- Palette: Sky blues, storm purples, lightning yellows
- Sky: Rolling layered clouds, purple lightning fork
- Ground: Island edges with waterfall curtains, rope bridges, windmills
- Vegetation: "Charged" crystal trees (leaf shimmer), glow-fruits
- Architecture: Air docks, tether masts, canvas tents, storm vanes

EARTHBOUND GIANTS (Mountains/Stone Fortresses):
- Environment: Mountain fortresses
- Exclusive Materials: Cryogem Ore, Flintsteel
- Palette: Rock grays, snow whites, moss greens
- Ground: Jagged cliff tiles, switchback paths, rope bridges, ice patches
- Vegetation: Hardy shrubs, alpine flowers, mushrooms, root vegetables, goat pens
- Architecture: Heavy stone walls, buttresses, triangular banners, forges (ember glow)

MYSTIC SCRIBES (Temples/Arcane Libraries):
- Environment: Libraries, temples
- Exclusive Materials: Aethercrystals, Soulshards
- Palette: Marble whites, golds, glowing blues
- Sky: Calm dusk gradients, floating lanterns
- Ground: Polished tiles, reflecting pools, trimmed hedges, runic pavements (pulse)
- Vegetation: Enchanted figs, golden pears, dreamleaf herbs, oversized blooms
- Architecture: Colonnades, domes, scroll banners, stained glass windows

SHADOW FOLK (Crypts/Wastelands):
- Environment: Crypts, wastelands
- Exclusive Materials: Shadowglass, Voidiron
- Palette: Black, gray, violet, crimson accents
- Sky: Low blood moon, fast mist drift
- Ground: Cracked earth, charcoal shrubs, thorn brambles, bone remnants
- Vegetation: Nightshade berries (poison icon), withered herbs
- Architecture: Black-stone keeps, iron spikes, chain bridges, braziers with violet fire

SPECIAL REALMS:
- Mythfall Gate (unlocked after Crown of the Seven Realms)
- Seasonal dungeons (rotating shard/gem rewards)
