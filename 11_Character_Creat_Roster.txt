=== CREAT ARCHETYPES BY ELEMENT ===

FIRE:
- Pyrogryph (fiery gryphon, ember wings, molten claws)
- Flame Wolf (wolf with flaming mane + ember breath)
- Ash Serpent (lava-scaled snake, leaves magma trails)

WATER:
- Tide Serpent (serpent with glowing fins)
- Coral Stag (antlers made of coral, walks on water's edge)
- Abyssal Turtle (massive armored shell, bioluminescent patterns)

EARTH:
- Moss Boar (stone tusks covered in vines, roots enemies)
- Terra Golem (humanoid stone giant, moss-crusted)
- Crystal Beetle (earthbound, armored, cracks earth underfoot)

STORM/LIGHTNING:
- Storm Roc (giant bird, feathers crackle with thunder)
- Thunder Hound (hound with electric paws, sparks trail)
- Sky Serpent (airborne snake, lightning strikes)

LIGHT:
- Radiant Stag (glowing antlers, white-gold aura)
- Sunhawk (falcon, blinding light attacks)
- Dawn Lion (golden mane, radiant roar)

SHADOW:
- Umbraven (shadow raven, violet glowing eyes)
- Phantom Lynx (shadowy lynx, phase-walk ability)
- Hollow Serpent (serpent that melts into darkness)

ICE/FROST:
- Frost Serpent (icy scales, frost mist aura)
- Glacial Bear (fur crackles with frost, heavy melee strikes)
- Shard Owl (crystalline feathers, screech slows foes)

ARCANE/MYSTIC:
- Aether Fox (multi-tailed, shimmering cosmic aura)
- Rune Elk (antlers carved with glowing runes)
- Star Moth (wings shine constellations, drains mana)

=== ENEMIES (NON-BOSS FOES) ===

- Emberlings: small fire imps in volcano regions
- Tideleeches: parasitic sea monsters in ocean zones
- Shadeborn Hounds: shadow wolf packs in wastelands
- Stone Sentinels: corrupted statues guarding ruins
- Sky Raiders: rogue riders with stolen creats
- Wraithbats: cave swarmers that drain stamina
- Frost Stalkers: ice wolves in mountain ranges
- Plague Crows: disease carriers in shadow zones
- Venom Spiders: web-spinners in forests
- Root Beasts: animated plants in overgrown areas

=== MAJOR BOSSES ===

STORY MODE:
- Ashthrone Veyla: Fire Queen, evolves (Fire → Frostflame → Emberstorm)
- Silkbrand Hollowshade: Shadow puppeteer, controls fallen riders
- Tidal Father Roen: Ocean giant who controls currents
- The Hollow Monarch: Final boss, wears shattered crown, commands undead creats

CROWN TRIALS:
- Solflare Aevan: Firebird with meteor divebombs
- Crystfang the Sealed: Ice elemental horror in glacial ruins
- Vexiloth: Storm serpent, mimics playstyle

ROAMING/SIDE QUEST:
- Thornmane: Nature boar, shifts terrain
- Skyrend: Betrayer wyvern-drake
- Froskarn: Ice-scorpion, freezes field

FACTION RAIDS:
- The False Sovereign: Corrupted former champion, glitch Crown
