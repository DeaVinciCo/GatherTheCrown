=== 2D VISUAL ASSETS & STYLE GUIDE ===

ENGINE TARGET:
- Flat 2D Python game (pygame)
- Readability > detail
- Very clear silhouettes
- Bold outline style (2–4 px dark outline)
- High-contrast shapes
- Limited but distinct palettes per biome
- Pixel scale target: 64x64 px tiles (primary), 128x128 variations for HD scenes

GLOBAL STYLE NOTES:
- Medieval-fantasy forest vibe with castles, ruins, inns, towns, shops, moats
- Do not clutter foreground
- Give readable negative space around interactive objects
- Keep biome palettes unique and instantly distinguishable
- Each region gets: map style + gameplay scene art + props + vegetation + palette

=== TEXTURE RULES ===

- Use large, hand-painted texture patterns (no tiny noise)
- 2–3 layers per surface: BASE color → MID micro-texture → EDGE highlights/shadows
- Hard ink outline (2–4 px) around key shapes
- Inner linework for key folds/scales/wood grain
- Avoid photo textures; everything stylized and consistent

=== CHARACTERS (RIDERS/NPCs) TEXTURES ===

- Skin: soft gradient shading; freckles/scars as single-stroke decals
- Hair: chunky locks; 2–3 tone streaks; clear silhouette shapes
- Cloth: visible weave (chevron/linen strokes); edge fray on low-tier gear
- Leather: big pore speckles + crease lines; darker seam lines
- Metal (armor): brushed streaks; rivet highlights; specular accents
- Fur/Capes: long directional strokes; hemline tatter on low-tier
- Faction markers: small sigils on shoulder/cloak
- Crown wear: metal sheen band on brow; socket glows by gem color

=== CREATS (MYTHICAL/MYSTICAL) TEXTURES ===

- Scales: big plate scales with beveled ink; alternating tone rows
- Feathers: layered clumps (3–5 per cluster); subtle barbs
- Hide/Fur: direction strokes; scar decals; darker ridge lines
- Stone/Crystal bodies: large facet planes; rim light on edges
- Elemental auras (always visible, light and readable):
  * FIRE: ember motes, orange rim light on edges
  * WATER: soft blue caustics, drip FX on steps
  * EARTH: dust flecks, cracked soil prints
  * STORM: tiny spark forks, after-image on dash
  * LIGHT: soft bloom at edges, white specks
  * SHADOW: violet haze, subtle blur mask
  * ICE: frost mist cone, sparkle flecks

=== ANIMATION NOTES ===

- Idle: subtle "breathing" (2–3 px scale pulse), cape/hair sway, aura loop
- Walk/Run: exaggerated silhouette motion; footstep FX by element
- Attack: 1–2 frame smear for readability; element burst frame with additive glow

=== LANDSCAPE TEXTURES ===

GRASS & GROUND:
- Grass tiles (64x64): 3 variants per biome with large blade clusters
- Dirt: big brush strokes; occasional pebbles; wheel rut decals
- Rock: broad planes, dark core cracks, lichen blobs

TREES & BUSHES:
- Trunks: spiral bark strokes; knot/branch sockets; moss strip decals
- Canopies: 3–4 big leaf clumps per tree
- Pines: banded needle clumps; snow-tipped version
- Willows: long rope leaves; water reflection decal under
- Bushes: berry color pops (blue/red); thorn outlines in shadow biomes

MOUNTAINS/CLIFFS:
- Tiered ledges; strata lines; snow masks at top
- Rope anchor decals for gameplay

WATER:
- Freshwater: calm ripple tile (8–10f loop); foam edge tiles; lily pad prop
- Ocean: wave crest tiles (12f); wet sand transition; tidepool diffraction
- Waterfalls: parallax layer with highlight streaks; mist puff at base

=== STRUCTURES TEXTURES ===

MATERIAL GUIDES:
- Stone blocks: hand-cut edges; mortar gaps; occasional chipped corners
- Timber frames: dark beams with wood grain; whitewash plaster with cracks
- Thatch roofs: big straw clumps, ridge cap pattern; darker wet patches
- Metalwork: rivets, hammered dimples; edge gleam; soot near forges
- Windows: lead lattice pattern; warm gradient glow; stained glass for temples

BUILD TYPES:
- Homes: 1–2 storey timber + stone foundation; sign hooks; stacked firewood
- Inns: swinging sign (8f); barrel clusters; lantern posts; porch benches
- Shops: awnings (striped cloth texture); crates; coin scales prop
- Abandoned homes: boarded windows, ivy takeover, collapsed roof holes
- Castles/Kingdoms: blockwork with buttresses, banners, portcullis mask
- Ruins: broken arches, fallen capitals, vine overlays, crown-relief blocks

=== PARALLAX LAYERS ===

Layer order (back → front):
- Sky (speed 0.10)
- Mountains/far (0.25)
- Distant trees/cliffs (0.45)
- Mid buildings/trees (0.65)
- Near props (0.85)
- Ground/actors (1.00)
- FX overlays (1.05)

Horizon kept low for "big sky" readability
Avoid busy skies in combat scenes

=== READABILITY RULES ===

DO:
- Keep silhouettes bold
- Avoid leaf clutter in front of actors
- Use thick outlines for interactive props and building entrances
- Keep water edges bright (foam) against darker land tiles

DON'T:
- Use heavy noise textures
- Exceed 6–7 major hues per biome
- Clutter foreground with decorative elements
