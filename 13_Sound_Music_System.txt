- 2D Python (pygame or pyglet audio support)
- Load assets in OGG or WAV (loop-safe)
- Compressed for size but HQ for clarity
- Categories: SFX, Ambience, Music, NPC Dialogue

=== SOUND RULES ===

- Loop ambience tracks seamlessly (crossfade)
- Play combat sounds layered (e.g., sword clash + grunt)
- NPC barks randomize between 2–3 versions to avoid repetition
- Music crossfades when moving between regions
- Fanfares (bells, trumpets, cheers) trigger on victories and crown completion

=== TRIGGERS ===

- Enter forest: play forest_day.ogg + forest_theme.ogg
- Enter inn: fade forest_theme → town_theme + innkeeper_greeting.ogg
- Combat start: fade out ambience 50%, raise combat_sfx + boss_battle_theme
- Crown forged: play crown_forge.ogg + crown_completion_fanfare.ogg
- Boss defeated: roar cut, crowd_cheer.ogg, loot sparkle
- Level up: level_up.ogg + visual effect
- Fast travel: whoosh sound + map unfurl

=== AUDIO SETTINGS ===

SLIDERS:
- Master Volume (0–100%)

ACCESSIBILITY:
