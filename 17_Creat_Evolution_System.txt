=== CREAT EVOLUTION SYSTEM ===

OVERVIEW:
- Creats evolve through 4 stages: Hatchling → Adolescent → Mature → Elder
- Evolution unlocks new abilities, stat boosts, and visual transformations
- Bond level and specific items required for evolution
- Element remains constant (Fire creat stays Fire)

=== EVOLUTION STAGES ===

STAGE 1: HATCHLING (Level 1-10)
- Starting form, small size
- Basic elemental attack
- 50% of max stats
- Visual: Compact, cute, glowing eyes
- Bond Required: 0% (starting stage)

STAGE 2: ADOLESCENT (Level 11-25)
- Medium size, more defined features
- Unlocks secondary ability
- 75% of max stats
- Visual: Larger, more aggressive features, brighter aura
- Bond Required: 40%
- Evolution Item: 10 Elemental Fruits + 500 GC

STAGE 3: MATURE (Level 26-45)
- Full adult size, battle-ready
- Unlocks Sync Mode compatibility
- 100% of max stats
- Visual: Fully developed, battle scars, intense aura
- Bond Required: 70%
- Evolution Item: 5 Rare Elemental Crystals + 5,000 GC

STAGE 4: ELDER (Level 46+)
- Legendary form, largest size
- Unlocks Ultimate Ability
- 125% of max stats (stat overflow)
- Visual: Ancient features, glowing runes, massive aura
- Bond Required: 95%
- Evolution Item: 1 Primal Essence (boss drop) + 50,000 GC

=== BOND SYSTEM ===

BOND METER (0-100%):
- Increases through: battles together, feeding, petting, training
- Decreases through: neglect, fainting in battle, using wrong element

BOND GAIN RATES:
- Win battle together: +2%
- Feed favorite food: +1%
- Pet/interact: +0.5% (once per day)
- Training session: +1%
- Sync Mode victory: +5%

BOND LOSS RATES:
- Creat faints in battle: -3%
- Don't feed for 3 days: -5%
- Use opposing element attack on creat: -10%

BOND MILESTONES:
- 25%: Creat learns your name (dialogue change)
- 50%: Unlocks combo attacks
- 75%: Creat protects you (auto-block 1 fatal hit per battle)
- 100%: Perfect Bond (stat boost, unique cosmetic aura)

=== EVOLUTION TRIGGERS ===

AUTOMATIC EVOLUTION:
- Reach level threshold + bond requirement
- Prompt appears: "Your creat is ready to evolve!"
- Player can choose to evolve now or delay

ITEM-REQUIRED EVOLUTION:
- Adolescent → Mature: 10 Elemental Fruits (element-specific)
- Mature → Elder: 5 Rare Elemental Crystals + 1 Primal Essence

SPECIAL EVOLUTIONS (Optional Variants):
- Shadow Fire Drake: Fire creat + Shadow Gem during evolution
- Frost Storm Roc: Storm creat + Ice Crystal during evolution
- Radiant Earth Golem: Earth creat + Light Shard during evolution

=== ELEMENTAL FRUITS (Evolution Items) ===

FIRE: Ember Fruit (volcano zones, fire bosses)
WATER: Tide Berry (ocean zones, water bosses)
EARTH: Stone Apple (mountain zones, earth bosses)
STORM: Thunder Grape (sky isles, storm bosses)
LIGHT: Radiant Pear (temples, light bosses)
SHADOW: Void Plum (crypts, shadow bosses)
ARCANE: Aether Fig (mystic libraries, arcane bosses)

DROP RATES:
- Common enemies: 5% chance
- Elite enemies: 15% chance
- Bosses: 50% chance (guaranteed on first kill)

=== RARE ELEMENTAL CRYSTALS ===

ACQUISITION:
- Boss drops (Tier 2-3): 25% chance
- Crown Trial completion: 1 guaranteed
- Faction raids: 2-3 per completion
- Merchant: 10,000 GC each (limited stock)

=== PRIMAL ESSENCE ===

ACQUISITION:
- Story Mode bosses (Level 3 only): 100% drop
- Faction raid final boss: 50% drop
- Crown of Seven Realms completion: 3 guaranteed

USES:
- Evolve creat to Elder stage
- Craft legendary weapons
- Unlock special cosmetics

- Speed: 15
- Special: Ember Breath (30 damage)

- Speed: 55 (+15)

=== EVOLUTION ANIMATION ===

SEQUENCE:
1. Creat glows brightly (2 seconds)
2. Light engulfs creat (white flash)
3. Silhouette grows/transforms (3 seconds)
4. Light fades, new form revealed
5. Creat roars/cries (element-specific sound)
6. Stat increase notification
7. New ability unlocked notification

SKIPPABLE: Yes (press Enter to skip)

=== DE-EVOLUTION / REGRESSION ===

NOT POSSIBLE:
- Once evolved, creat cannot revert to previous stage
- This is permanent progression

EXCEPTION:
- If bond drops below 20%, creat becomes "Feral"
- Feral state: refuses commands, attacks randomly, visual corruption
- Fix: Restore bond to 30%+ through feeding and care

=== SPECIAL EVOLUTION VARIANTS ===

HYBRID EVOLUTIONS (Rare):
- Require specific gem during evolution
- Changes appearance and adds secondary element
- Example: Fire Drake + Shadow Gem = Shadow Flame Drake
  * Retains fire attacks
  * Gains shadow stealth ability
  * Visual: Black scales with ember cracks

AVAILABLE HYBRIDS:
- Fire + Shadow = Shadow Flame
- Water + Ice = Frost Tide
- Earth + Light = Radiant Stone
- Storm + Fire = Inferno Tempest
- Light + Arcane = Celestial Radiance
- Shadow + Void = Abyssal Void

HYBRID REQUIREMENTS:
- Mature stage evolution only
- Specific gem (Alexandrite, Painite, or element-specific rare gem)
- Bond 80%+
- 25,000 GC

=== EVOLUTION STRATEGY TIPS ===

EARLY GAME:
- Focus on bond building (feed, battle, interact)
- Save Elemental Fruits for Adolescent evolution
- Don't rush evolution (higher level = better base stats)

MID GAME:
- Farm Rare Elemental Crystals from Crown Trials
- Decide if you want standard or hybrid evolution
- Mature stage unlocks Sync Mode (essential for Story bosses)

LATE GAME:
- Prioritize Primal Essence farming (Story Mode Level 3 bosses)
- Elder evolution is endgame power spike
- Consider multiple creats at Elder stage for versatility

=== CREAT RETIREMENT SYSTEM ===

OPTION: Retire creat to Sanctuary
- Frees up creat slot
- Creat remains in Sanctuary (can visit, but not battle)
- Keeps bond level and cosmetics
- Can be recalled for 10,000 GC (once per creat)

PURPOSE:
- Allows experimentation with new creats
- Doesn't punish players for trying different elements
- Preserves emotional attachment

SANCTUARY LOCATION:
- Mystic Scribes faction zone
- Peaceful meadow with elemental habitats
- NPCs care for retired creats
