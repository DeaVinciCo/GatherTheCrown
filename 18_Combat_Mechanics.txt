=== COMBAT MECHANICS ===

COMBAT STYLE: Real-Time Action RPG
- Player controls character movement and attacks
- Creat acts as AI companion (can issue commands)
- Dodge, block, and combo systems
- Stamina and mana management

=== CORE COMBAT LOOP ===

1. ENGAGE: Enter combat zone or trigger boss
2. POSITION: Move to optimal range (melee/ranged)
3. ATTACK: Execute combos, manage stamina
4. DEFEND: Dodge/block enemy attacks
5. SYNC: Build sync meter for ultimate attacks
6. VICTORY: Defeat enemy, collect loot

=== PLAYER CONTROLS ===

MOVEMENT:
- WASD or Arrow Keys: Move character
- Shift: Sprint (consumes stamina)
- Space: Dodge roll (i-frames, costs 20 stamina)

COMBAT:
- Left Click: Light attack (fast, low damage)
- Right Click: Heavy attack (slow, high damage)
- Q: Special ability (weapon-specific)
- E: Use equipped item (potion, bomb, etc.)
- R: Block (hold, drains stamina while active)

CREAT COMMANDS:
- 1: Attack target
- 2: Defend me
- 3: Use special ability
- 4: Retreat/heal

SYNC MODE:
- F: Activate Sync Mode (requires 100% sync meter)

=== STAMINA SYSTEM ===

MAX STAMINA: 100 (base, increases with stats)

STAMINA COSTS:
- Light attack: 5 stamina
- Heavy attack: 15 stamina
- Dodge roll: 20 stamina
- Sprint: 10 stamina per second
- Block: 5 stamina per second

STAMINA REGEN:
- Idle: 10 stamina per second
- Walking: 5 stamina per second
- Blocking: 0 stamina per second
- Sprinting: 0 stamina per second

EXHAUSTED STATE:
- Stamina reaches 0: character slows, attacks weakened
- Cannot dodge or sprint
- Lasts 3 seconds or until stamina reaches 30

=== MANA SYSTEM ===

MAX MANA: 100 (base, increases with Wisdom stat)

MANA COSTS:
- Basic spell: 20 mana
- Elemental attack: 30 mana
- Healing spell: 40 mana
- Ultimate spell: 100 mana

MANA REGEN:
- Idle: 5 mana per second
- Combat: 2 mana per second
- Meditation (out of combat): 15 mana per second

MANA POTIONS:
- Small: +30 mana (instant)
- Medium: +60 mana (instant)
- Large: +100 mana (instant)

=== HEALTH SYSTEM ===

MAX HEALTH: Varies by character level and Stamina stat
- Level 1: 100 HP
- Level 50: 500 HP
- Elder creat bonus: +100 HP

DAMAGE TYPES:
- Physical: Swords, axes, blunt weapons
- Elemental: Fire, ice, lightning, shadow, light
- True damage: Ignores armor (rare)

ARMOR/DEFENSE:
- Reduces physical damage by %
- Formula: Damage Reduction = Armor / (Armor + 100)
- Example: 50 armor = 33% damage reduction

ELEMENTAL RESISTANCE:
- Each element has separate resistance stat
- Fire resistance reduces fire damage
- Wearing Fire crown: +20% fire resistance

HEALING:
- Health potions: Instant heal
- Creat healing: Over time (Water/Light creats)
- Campfire rest: Full heal (out of combat)
- Inn rest: Full heal + buffs

DEATH:
- Player HP reaches 0: Respawn at last checkpoint
- Lose 10% of current gold (not total)
- Creat HP reaches 0: Creat faints, bond -3%, respawns after battle

=== COMBO SYSTEM ===

LIGHT ATTACK COMBOS:
- 3 light attacks = 3-hit combo (bonus damage on 3rd hit)
- Timing window: 1 second between attacks

HEAVY ATTACK COMBOS:
- Light → Light → Heavy = Launcher (knocks enemy up)
- Heavy → Light → Light = Sweep (AoE)

ELEMENTAL COMBOS:
- Fire attack → Lightning attack = Overload (explosion)
- Ice attack → Fire attack = Steam Cloud (blinds enemies)
- Shadow attack → Light attack = Void Rift (high damage)

CREAT COMBOS:
- Player attack + Creat attack (within 0.5 sec) = Sync Strike (+50% damage)

COMBO COUNTER:
- Displays on screen (e.g., "5 HIT COMBO!")
- Resets if 2 seconds pass without attack
- Bonus damage: +5% per combo hit (max +50% at 10 hits)

=== DODGE & BLOCK MECHANICS ===

DODGE ROLL:
- 0.5 seconds of invincibility frames (i-frames)
- Moves character 2 meters in movement direction
- Costs 20 stamina
- Cooldown: 1 second

PERFECT DODGE:
- Dodge within 0.2 seconds of enemy attack
- Triggers slow-motion (1 second)
- Next attack deals +100% damage
- Refunds 10 stamina

BLOCK:
- Hold R to raise shield/weapon
- Reduces damage by 50-80% (depends on shield quality)
- Drains stamina while active
- Heavy attacks break block (stagger)

PERFECT BLOCK:
- Block within 0.3 seconds of enemy attack
- Negates all damage
- Staggers enemy (2 seconds)
- Refunds stamina cost

STAGGER:
- Enemy cannot attack or move
- Take +50% damage
- Duration: 2-4 seconds (depends on enemy size)

=== CREAT AI BEHAVIOR ===

STANCE MODES:
- AGGRESSIVE: Creat attacks on sight, uses abilities frequently
- DEFENSIVE: Creat stays near player, blocks attacks
- BALANCED: Mix of offense and defense (default)

CREAT ACTIONS:
- Basic attack: Elemental bite/claw/breath
- Special ability: Element-specific (fireball, ice shard, etc.)
- Sync attack: Coordinated attack with player
- Protect: Blocks fatal hit (once per battle, 75%+ bond)

CREAT TARGETING:
- Prioritizes player's target
- Switches to nearest enemy if player target dies
- Retreats if HP below 20%

CREAT COOLDOWNS:
- Basic attack: No cooldown
- Special ability: 10 seconds
- Sync attack: 30 seconds (or when sync meter full)

=== SYNC MODE ===

SYNC METER:
- Builds from 0-100% during combat
- Gain: +5% per player attack hit
- Gain: +10% per creat attack hit
- Gain: +20% per combo finisher
- Resets to 0 after use

ACTIVATION:
- Press F when meter at 100%
- Player and creat merge auras (3 seconds)
- Invincible during activation

SYNC MODE EFFECTS:
- Duration: 15 seconds
- +50% damage for player and creat
- +30% movement speed
- Unlimited stamina
- Creat abilities have no cooldown
- Ends with Ultimate Sync Attack

ULTIMATE SYNC ATTACK:
- Massive damage (500-1000 depending on level)
- Element-specific animation
- AoE or single-target (depends on creat type)
- Guaranteed crit

EXAMPLES:
- Fire Drake: Meteor Dive (AoE, burn effect)
- Water Serpent: Tidal Wave (AoE, knockback)
- Earth Golem: Earthquake (AoE, stun)
- Storm Roc: Lightning Storm (multi-target)
- Shadow Panther: Void Slash (single-target, massive damage)

=== ENEMY AI ===

BASIC ENEMIES:
- Simple attack patterns (1-2 attacks)
- Predictable timing
- Low HP (1-3 hits to kill)

ELITE ENEMIES:
- 3-4 attack patterns
- Dodge player attacks occasionally
- Medium HP (5-10 hits)
- Drop better loot

MINI-BOSSES:
- 5+ attack patterns
- Telegraphed attacks (visual cues)
- Phase changes at 50% HP
- High HP (20-30 hits)

STORY BOSSES:
- Complex attack patterns (10+ moves)
- Multiple phases (3-5)
- Environmental hazards
- Enrage mechanics
- Very high HP (50-100+ hits)

ATTACK TELEGRAPHS:
- Red glow: Heavy attack incoming (1 second warning)
- Yellow glow: Special ability (2 second warning)
- Purple glow: AoE attack (3 second warning, shows radius)

=== ELEMENTAL INTERACTIONS ===

WEAKNESS CHART:
- Fire > Ice (2x damage)
- Ice > Earth (2x damage)
- Earth > Lightning (2x damage)
- Lightning > Water (2x damage)
- Water > Fire (2x damage)
- Light > Shadow (2x damage)
- Shadow > Light (2x damage)
- Arcane = Neutral (1x to all)

STATUS EFFECTS:
- Burn: 10 damage per second, 5 seconds
- Freeze: Cannot move, 3 seconds
- Stun: Cannot attack, 3 seconds
- Poison: 5 damage per second, 10 seconds
- Blind: Reduced accuracy, 5 seconds
- Slow: -50% movement speed, 5 seconds

ELEMENTAL SHIELDS:
- Bosses can activate elemental shields
- Shield absorbs damage from non-weakness elements
- Must use weakness element to break shield
- Example: Fire boss has fire shield, use ice to break

=== DIFFICULTY SCALING ===

EASY MODE:
- Enemies deal 50% damage
- Player deals 150% damage
- Longer dodge i-frames (0.7 seconds)
- More forgiving perfect dodge/block windows

NORMAL MODE:
- Standard damage values
- Default i-frames and timing windows

HARD MODE:
- Enemies deal 150% damage
- Player deals 75% damage
- Shorter i-frames (0.3 seconds)
- Tighter perfect dodge/block windows
- Enemies have more HP

NIGHTMARE MODE (Unlocked after Story completion):
- Enemies deal 200% damage
- Player deals 50% damage
- No i-frames on dodge (must position correctly)
- Enemies have 2x HP
- Bosses have additional attack patterns
- Rewards: 2x gold, 2x gem drops, exclusive cosmetics

=== COMBAT REWARDS ===

PER ENEMY KILL:
- Gold coins (5-50 GC)
- Experience points (levels up character and creat)
- Chance for item drops (weapons, food, gems)

COMBO BONUSES:
- 10+ combo: +10% gold
- 20+ combo: +20% gold, +5% item drop rate
- 30+ combo: +30% gold, +10% item drop rate, guaranteed rare drop

PERFECT COMBAT (No damage taken):
- +50% gold
- +20% item drop rate
- Bonus: 1 Elemental Fruit

SYNC MODE FINISH:
- +25% gold
- Guaranteed gem drop
- Bonus: Crown shard (if in crown zone)

=== COMBAT ACCESSIBILITY ===

OPTIONS:
- Auto-dodge: Automatically dodges when enemy attacks (cooldown applies)
- Auto-block: Automatically blocks heavy attacks
- Slow-motion mode: Combat runs at 50% speed
- Aim assist: Attacks auto-target nearest enemy
- Simplified combos: 3 light attacks always triggers combo finisher

VISUAL AIDS:
- Enemy attack indicators (arrows, circles)
- Colorblind-friendly status effect icons
- Large damage numbers
- Screen shake toggle

AUDIO CUES:
- Sound effect for enemy attack wind-up
- Different sounds for light/heavy attacks
- Audio cue for perfect dodge/block timing

=== COMBAT TIPS (In-Game Tutorial) ===

1. "Watch for red glows - that's your cue to dodge!"
2. "Perfect blocks stagger enemies, opening them up for combos."
3. "Build your sync meter by landing hits with both you and your creat."
4. "Elemental weaknesses deal double damage - use them!"
5. "Running out of stamina leaves you vulnerable. Manage it wisely."
6. "Creat bond affects their performance. Keep them happy!"
7. "Combo finishers deal bonus damage. Chain your attacks!"
8. "Some bosses require specific elements to break their shields."

=== COMBAT ZONES ===

OPEN WORLD:
- Enemies roam freely
- Can avoid combat by sneaking
- Aggro range: 5 meters

DUNGEONS:
- Enemies patrol set paths
- Cannot leave combat once engaged
- Locked doors until all enemies defeated

ARENAS:
- Waves of enemies
- Timed challenges
- Leaderboards for fastest clear

BOSS ARENAS:
- Locked arena (cannot leave)
- Environmental hazards (lava, spikes, falling rocks)
- Phase transitions change arena layout

=== COMBAT PROGRESSION ===

EARLY GAME (Levels 1-10):
- Learn basic attacks and dodging
- Simple enemy patterns
- Tutorial bosses with clear telegraphs

MID GAME (Levels 11-30):
- Unlock combo system
- Enemies use status effects
- Bosses have 2-3 phases

LATE GAME (Levels 31-50):
- Master Sync Mode
- Complex enemy patterns
- Bosses require elemental strategy

ENDGAME (Level 50+):
- Perfect dodge/block required
- Nightmare mode challenges
- Faction raids with team coordination
