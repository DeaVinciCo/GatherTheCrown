## 📊 PROGRESSION SYSTEMS

### Player Level (1-99)

**XP Sources:**
- Races: 500-2,000 XP
- Quests: 1,000-5,000 XP
- Bosses: 3,000-10,000 XP
- Exploration: 100-500 XP
- PvP: 1,000-3,000 XP

**Level Rewards:**
```
LEVEL MILESTONES:
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Level 5:  Unlock PvP                   │
│ Level 10: Unlock breeding              │
│ Level 15: Unlock Act 2                 │
│ Level 20: Unlock advanced training     │
│ Level 30: Unlock Act 3                 │
│ Level 40: Unlock specialization        │
│ Level 50: Unlock legendary gear        │
│ Level 60: Unlock Act 4                 │
│ Level 75: Unlock mythic content        │
│ Level 99: Unlock prestige system       │
└────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**XP Curve:**
- Levels 1-20: 1,000 XP per level (fast)
- Levels 21-50: 5,000 XP per level (medium)
- Levels 51-80: 15,000 XP per level (slow)
- Levels 81-99: 30,000 XP per level (very slow)

---

### Prestige System (Level 99+)

**Prestige Options:**

**Option 1: Rebirth**
- Reset to level 1
- Keep 50% of stats
- Unlock new evolution paths
- Gain prestige star (cosmetic)
- Bonus: +10% XP gain permanently

**Option 2: Ascension**
- Stay at level 99
- Unlock mythic-tier content
- Gain ascension aura (cosmetic)
- Bonus: +20% all stats

**Option 3: Legacy**
- Retire character
- Pass traits to new character
- Unlock special breed
- Gain legacy title
- Bonus: New character starts level 10

**Prestige Levels:**
- Can prestige up to 10 times
- Each prestige: Harder but better rewards
- Prestige 10: "Eternal" title, max bonuses

---

### Faction Reputation (5 Ranks)

**Rank 1: Initiate (0-999 rep)**
- Basic faction quests
- Faction emblem
- 5% discount at faction shops

**Rank 2: Member (1,000-2,999 rep)**
- Intermediate quests
- Faction gear (common-rare)
- 10% discount

**Rank 3: Champion (3,000-5,999 rep)**
- Advanced quests
- Faction gear (epic)
- 15% discount
- Faction mount

**Rank 4: Elite (6,000-9,999 rep)**
- Elite quests
- Faction gear (legendary)
- 20% discount
- Faction ability upgrade

**Rank 5: Legend (10,000+ rep)**
- Legendary quests
- Faction gear (mythic)
- 25% discount
- Faction title
- Faction home

**Reputation Gain:**
- Faction quests: 50-500 rep
- Faction events: 100-1,000 rep
- Donations: 1 rep per 10g
- PvP (faction wars): 25-100 rep

## 🎯 DAILY/WEEKLY/SEASONAL GOALS

### Daily Goals (Reset 24 hours)
- Win 3 races: 500g, 1,000 XP
- Complete 5 quests: 750g, 1,500 XP
- Train creat 3 times: 300g, 500 XP
- Win 1 PvP match: 400g, 1,000 XP

**Daily Bonus:** Complete all 4 = +1 Crown Shard

---

### Weekly Goals (Reset 7 days)
- Win 25 races: 5,000g, 10,000 XP
- Defeat 10 bosses: 7,500g, 15,000 XP
- Complete 20 quests: 10,000g, 20,000 XP
- Earn 50,000g: 5,000g bonus, rare item

**Weekly Bonus:** Complete all 4 = +5 Crown Shards, epic item

---

### Seasonal Goals (Reset 3 months)
- Reach level 80: Legendary item
- Win 500 races: Exclusive mount skin
- Max faction reputation: Faction mythic gear
- Earn 1,000,000g: "Millionaire" title

**Seasonal Bonus:** Complete all 4 = +50 Crown Shards, mythic item, seasonal title
