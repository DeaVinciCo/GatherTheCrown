# Attack System - Complete Reference

## 📋 Attack Roster (15 Total)

### 🔺 Damage Attacks

**1. Blazing Charge** (Fire)
- Base Damage: 20 HP
- Effect: Dash forward with knockback + burn trail (3 HP/sec for 3s)
- Uses: 3
- Duration: Instant dash + 3s burn
- Counter: Ice shield, dodge roll
- Unlock: Fire Temple side quest

**2. Frost Fangs** (Ice)
- Base Damage: 15 HP
- Effect: Freeze target's mount for 2 seconds
- Uses: 4
- Duration: 2s freeze
- Counter: Fire resistance, rapid button mash
- Unlock: Glacier Serpent boss drop

**3. Rock Spike** (Earth)
- Base Damage: 25 HP
- Effect: Ground spike erupts under target
- Uses: 3
- Duration: Instant
- Counter: Air jump, speed boost away
- Unlock: Deeproot Range quest

### 🌀 Utility Attacks

**4. Wind Breaker** (Air)
- Base Damage: 10 HP
- Effect: Launches opponent back + drops their item
- Uses: 4
- Duration: Instant
- Counter: Heavy mount (Earth type), anchor stance
- Unlock: Storm Vulture bond level 5

**5. Spectral Cloak** (Shadow)
- Base Damage: 0 HP
- Effect: Go invisible for 3 seconds (pass through attacks)
- Uses: 2
- Duration: 3s
- Counter: Light pulse reveals, tracker abilities
- Unlock: Echo Trial side quest

**6. Mirage Field** (Illusion)
- Base Damage: 0 HP
- Effect: Creates 2 false copies of your rider for 5 seconds
- Uses: 2
- Duration: 5s
- Counter: AOE attacks hit all copies
- Unlock: Sand Maze completion

### ⚡ Speed Attacks

**7. Wind Surge** (Air)
- Base Damage: 0 HP
- Effect: 2x speed boost for 4 seconds
- Uses: 3
- Duration: 4s
- Counter: Terrain traps, oil slicks
- Unlock: Starting ability for Air class

**8. Lightning Glide** (Electric)
- Base Damage: 5 HP to nearby enemies
- Effect: Speed boost + stuns closest enemy for 1s
- Uses: 3
- Duration: 1s stun
- Counter: Grounded (Earth type), rubber armor
- Unlock: Azure Heights merchant (500g)

### 🛡️ Defense Attacks

**9. Stone Shell** (Earth)
- Base Damage: 0 HP
- Effect: Absorbs next 30 HP of damage
- Uses: 2
- Duration: Until broken or 10s
- Counter: Armor-piercing attacks, sustained DOT
- Unlock: Stonehorn bond level 3

**10. Life Vine** (Nature)
- Base Damage: 0 HP
- Effect: Heal 20 HP over 4 seconds
- Uses: 2
- Duration: 4s
- Counter: Fire burns vines (cancels heal)
- Unlock: Rootguard faction quest

**11. Echo Pulse** (Light)
- Base Damage: 0 HP
- Effect: Reflects next attack back to sender
- Uses: 1
- Duration: 2s window
- Counter: Don't attack during pulse, wait it out
- Unlock: Legendary Path completion

### 💎 Economy Attacks

**12. Gem Drain** (Magic)
- Base Damage: 5 HP
- Effect: Steal 50-100 gold from nearest player
- Uses: 3
- Duration: Instant
- Counter: Distance, vault lock item
- Unlock: Black Market Trader (rare spawn)

**13. Gold Siphon** (Curse)
- Base Damage: 0 HP
- Effect: Target loses 10g per second for 5 seconds (you gain it)
- Uses: 2
- Duration: 5s
- Counter: Cleanse, break line of sight
- Unlock: Crownless faction quest

### 🧩 Terrain Manipulation

**14. Mud Snare** (Earth)
- Base Damage: 0 HP
- Effect: Sticky trap that slows enemy by 50% for 3s if stepped on
- Uses: 5
- Duration: Trap lasts 15s or until triggered
- Counter: Air jump over, water washes away
- Unlock: Starting ability for Earth class

**15. Meteor Call** (Ultimate - Fire)
- Base Damage: 40 HP
- Effect: Falling fireballs target 1st place rider
- Uses: 1
- Duration: 3s barrage
- Counter: Shelter under terrain, shield
- Unlock: Molten Crownling (World Boss) drop

## 🎯 Core/Static Attacks (Class-Based)

These are locked-in for the entire race based on character + creature pairing.

### Fire Beast Rider
**Pyroclaw Leap**
- Jump attack that ignites enemy + terrain
- 3s burn trail left behind
- 4 uses per race

### Shadow Rogue
**Tether Lash**
- Pulls closest racer back to you
- 15 HP damage + 2s stun
- 3 uses per race

### Nature Monk
**Myst Leaf Trail**
- Leaves healing trail behind you (5 HP/sec for allies)
- Lasts 5 seconds
- 4 uses per race

### Tech Rider
**Volta Pulse**
- Speed burst that disables enemy boosts on contact
- 10 HP damage
- 5 uses per race

---

## 🔄 Mid-Race Pickup Attacks

These appear as glowing orbs/shards on the track. Random selection from unused attack pool.

**Pickup Types:**
- 🔴 Red Orb = Damage attack
- 🔵 Blue Orb = Defense/Utility
- 🟡 Yellow Orb = Speed boost
- 🟣 Purple Orb = Economy/Terrain
- ⚪ White Orb = Random (any category)

**Pickup Rules:**
- Max 5 attacks equipped at once
- Must drop one of your 4 swappable attacks to pick up new one
- Cannot drop Core/Static attack
- Missed enemy attacks leave behind 1-use versions (if you have affinity)
