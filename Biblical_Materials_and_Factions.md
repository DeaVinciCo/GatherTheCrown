## 🧶 FIBERS & FABRICS (Crafting Materials)

### Wool
- **Source:** Sheep, goats
- **Properties:** Warmth, insulation, water-resistant
- **Uses:** Winter gear, heavy armor padding, priestly robes
- **Gameplay Buffs:** +15% cold resistance, +10% defense
- **Harvest Locations:** Sylvara (Forest Kingdom), pastoral zones
- **Rarity:** Common

### Linen (Flax)
- **Source:** Flax plants
- **Properties:** Breathable, cooling, lightweight
- **Uses:** Summer clothing, purity robes, temple garments
- **Gameplay Buffs:** +20% agility, +10% spirit energy
- **Harvest Locations:** Zerath Dunes (Desert Kingdom), river valleys
- **Rarity:** Common

### Silk
- **Source:** Silkworms (mentioned in Ezekiel 16:10, 13)
- **Properties:** Luxurious, smooth, royal fabric
- **Uses:** Royal outfits, high-tier armor linings, trade goods
- **Gameplay Buffs:** +15% charisma, +10% magic resistance
- **Harvest Locations:** Luminaris (Crystal Kingdom), rare merchant drops
- **Rarity:** Rare

### Goat Hair
- **Source:** Goats (used in Tabernacle coverings, Exodus 26:7)
- **Properties:** Durable, weather-resistant
- **Uses:** Tents, travel gear, nomad equipment
- **Gameplay Buffs:** +25% durability, +10% weather resistance
- **Harvest Locations:** Mountain regions, nomad camps
- **Rarity:** Uncommon

### Cotton (Historical overlap with "fine linen")
- **Source:** Cotton plants
- **Properties:** Soft, breathable, versatile
- **Uses:** Everyday clothing, bandages, light armor
- **Gameplay Buffs:** +10% comfort (reduces stamina drain)
- **Harvest Locations:** Mor'gahl Fen (Swamp Kingdom), farmlands
- **Rarity:** Common

## ⚠️ SHATNEZ LAW (Crafting Restriction)

**Biblical Rule:** Deuteronomy 22:11 & Leviticus 19:19  
"You shall not wear a garment of divers sorts, as of wool and linen together."

### In-Game Mechanic
- **Forbidden Combo:** Wool + Linen in same garment
- **Consequence:** Cursed item with debuffs
  - -20% all stats
  - Attracts hostile enemies
  - Cannot be blessed or enchanted
  - Glows with dark aura (visible to other players)
- **Warning System:** Crafting UI shows red warning when attempting forbidden mix
- **Lore Explanation:** "The mixing of animal and plant fibers disrupts divine order"

### Allowed Combinations
✅ Wool + Silk  
✅ Wool + Cotton  
✅ Wool + Goat Hair  
✅ Linen + Silk  
✅ Linen + Cotton  
✅ Silk + Cotton  
✅ Any single fiber alone

|-------|-------|-------|--------|
| Sardius (Ruby) | Reuben | Red | +20% fire damage |

| Sapphire | Issachar | Blue | +20% wisdom/mana |
| Diamond | Zebulun | Clear | +25% all resistances |
| Ligure (Jacinth) | Dan | Orange | +15% agility |
| Agate | Naphtali | Banded | +20% endurance |
| Amethyst | Gad | Purple | +15% magic power |
| Beryl | Asher | Sea-green | +20% water affinity |
| Onyx | Joseph | Black/white | +15% balance/harmony |

- **Harvest:** Boss drops, mining (extremely rare), royal vaults

- Guardians of divine law and order
- Disciplined warriors and scholars
- Keepers of ancient knowledge

**Gameplay Style:**
- Defensive/support class
- High wisdom and perception
- Tank and crowd control specialists

**Exclusive Materials:**
- Silver (primary metal)
- Blue dye (tekhelet)
- Sapphire (tribal stone)
- Linen garments

**Starting Bonuses:**
- +20% defense
- +15% wisdom
- +10% perception range
- Immune to confusion effects

**Faction Abilities:**
- **Divine Shield:** Create protective barrier for allies
- **Lawkeeper's Gaze:** Reveal hidden enemies and traps
- **Covenant Bond:** Share buffs with nearby faction members

**Faction Quests:**
- Recover lost scrolls of law
- Defend temples from corruption
- Train new initiates in discipline

- Follow Biblical material rules = blessed items

- Mix wool + linen = cursed items

- **Watchers:** Bezalel (Exodus 31) - master of all crafts

## 🎮 GAMEPLAY INTEGRATION

### Character Creation
1. Choose faction (determines starting bonuses)
2. Select primary material affinity
3. Customize appearance with faction colors
4. Receive starter gear (faction-appropriate)

### Progression
- Level up within faction ranks
- Unlock exclusive crafting recipes
- Gain access to faction-only zones
- Earn faction-specific titles and cosmetics

### Endgame
- Become faction leader
- Unite or conquer other factions
- Rebuild the Temple (requires all factions)
- Craft the ultimate "Breastplate of Judgment"
