## 🎨 CHARACTER CREATION

### Step 1: Basic Identity

**Name:**
- Text input (3-20 characters)
- Profanity filter
- Uniqueness check (per server)
- Can change later (500g fee)

**Pronouns:**
- He/Him
- She/Her
- They/Them
- Custom input (respectful)
- Affects dialogue and NPC interactions

**Voice:**
- 6 voice options (3 per traditional gender)
- Pitch slider (low to high)
- Preview dialogue samples
- Can change later (free)

---

### Step 2: Physical Appearance

**Body Type:**
- Slim
- Athletic
- Muscular
- Stocky
- Curvy
- Plus-size

**Height:**
- Slider: 4'10" to 6'8" (147cm to 203cm)
- Affects character model scale
- No gameplay impact

**Skin Tone:**
- 30+ realistic tones
- Full spectrum (very light to very dark)
- Undertones: Cool, warm, neutral
- Custom RGB slider (advanced)

**Age Appearance:**
- Young (16-20)
- Adult (21-35)
- Mature (36-50)
- Elder (51+)
- Affects facial features, not stats

**Mouth:**
- Lip shape: 12 options
- Lip size: Slider (thin to full)
- Lip color: Natural tones + fantasy colors
- Teeth: Normal, fangs, tusks (fantasy)

**Ears:**
- Human: 8 shapes
- Elf: Pointed (3 lengths)
- Fantasy: Animal ears (unlockable)
