## 🥚 CREAT LIFECYCLE

### Stage 1: Egg (Level 0)
**Duration:** Until hatched (player choice)

**Egg Types by Element:**
- 🔥 Fire Egg: Glowing ember, warm to touch, smoke wisps
- 💧 Water Egg: Translucent blue, bubbles inside, cool surface
- 🌪️ Air Egg: Floating slightly, feather-light, wind swirls
- 🪨 Earth Egg: Rocky shell, moss growing, heavy and solid
- ❄️ Ice Egg: Frosted surface, cold mist, crystalline patterns
- ☠️ Poison Egg: Green veins, toxic smell, pulsing glow
- ☀️ Light Egg: Radiant gold, warm light, humming sound
- 🌑 Shadow Egg: Dark purple, absorbs light, whispers

**Hatching Requirements:**
- **Time:** 24 real-world hours OR 7 in-game days
- **Conditions:** Must be in appropriate environment
  - Fire eggs: Near lava/fire
  - Water eggs: Near water/rain
  - Ice eggs: In cold zones
  - Etc.
- **Ritual:** Player must perform hatching ceremony
  - Place egg on altar
  - Use faction-appropriate blessing
  - Speak creat's name (player chooses)

**Pre-Hatch Bonding:**
- Carry egg in inventory (+1 bond/hour)
- Keep in appropriate temperature (+2 bond/hour)
- Talk to egg (dialogue options) (+5 bond per conversation)
- Use sacred oils on shell (+10 bond)

---

### Stage 2: Hatchling (Levels 1-10)
**Duration:** ~5-8 hours of gameplay

**Appearance:**
- Small, cute, vulnerable
- Big eyes, clumsy movements
- Can't be ridden yet (follows player on foot)
- Elemental features just beginning to show

**Abilities:**
- **Basic Attack:** Weak elemental bite (10 damage)
- **Follow:** Stays close to player
- **Fetch:** Can retrieve small items
- **Emote:** Playful animations (nuzzle, play, sleep)

**Bonding Activities:**
- **Feed:** Give food 3x per day (+5 bond each)
- **Play:** Mini-games (fetch, hide-seek) (+10 bond)
- **Pet:** Physical affection (+3 bond per pet)
- **Train:** Basic commands (+8 bond per session)
- **Sleep Together:** Rest at camp (+15 bond)

**Growth Triggers:**
- Reach Level 10
- Bond Level 25+
- Complete "First Steps" quest
- Feed evolution stone (optional, speeds up)

---

### Stage 3: Juvenile (Levels 11-30)
**Duration:** ~15-20 hours of gameplay

**Appearance:**
- Medium size, now rideable
- Awkward teenage phase (gangly, uncoordinated)
- Elemental features more pronounced
- Saddle can be equipped

**Abilities:**
- **Mounted Combat:** Can fight while riding
- **Elemental Breath:** Basic elemental attack (30 damage)
- **Dash:** Short burst of speed
- **Loyalty Ability:** Protects player when HP low

**Bonding Activities:**
- **Race Together:** Complete races (+15 bond per race)
- **Combat Training:** Fight enemies (+10 bond per battle)
- **Grooming:** Clean and care for creat (+12 bond)
- **Exploration:** Discover new areas together (+20 bond)
- **Tricks:** Teach new moves (+15 bond per trick)

**Customization Unlocked:**
- Saddle designs (affects stats)
- Armor pieces (head, body, legs)
- Cosmetic dyes (faction colors)
- Accessories (scarves, jewelry, war paint)

**Growth Triggers:**
- Reach Level 30
- Bond Level 75+
- Complete "Coming of Age" trial
- Witness major story event together

---

### Stage 4: Adult (Levels 31-60)
**Duration:** ~30-40 hours of gameplay

**Appearance:**
- Full size, powerful, majestic
- Elemental features fully developed
- Battle scars and personality show
- Unique markings based on player choices

**Abilities:**
- **Ultimate Attack:** Powerful elemental ability (100+ damage)
- **Elemental Immunity:** Immune to own element
- **Advanced Movement:** Flight, swimming, wall-climbing (species dependent)
- **Bond Ability:** Unique skill based on relationship
  - High bond: Resurrection (revive player once per day)
  - Medium bond: Shield (protect from fatal blow)
  - Low bond: Flee (runs away when scared)

**Bonding Activities:**
- **Legendary Hunts:** Take down world bosses (+50 bond)
- **Kingdom Restoration:** Clear vaults together (+40 bond)
- **PvP Battles:** Compete in arenas (+25 bond per win)
- **Meditation:** Sync minds at shrines (+30 bond)
- **Breeding:** Introduce to mate (if applicable) (+100 bond)

**Specialization Choice (Level 40):**
Player chooses creat's path:

**Path A: Warrior**
- +30% attack damage
- +20% physical defense
- Learns aggressive abilities
- Appearance: Battle scars, armor plating

**Path B: Speedster**
- +40% movement speed
- +25% agility
- Learns mobility abilities
- Appearance: Sleek, aerodynamic

**Path C: Tank**
- +50% HP
- +35% defense
- Learns protective abilities
- Appearance: Bulky, armored

**Path D: Mage**
- +40% elemental damage
- +30% mana pool
- Learns magical abilities
- Appearance: Glowing runes, mystical aura

---

### Stage 5: Elder (Levels 61-99)
**Duration:** Endgame content

**Appearance:**
- Legendary status, awe-inspiring
- Elemental mastery visible (constant aura)
- Scars tell story of adventures
- Respected by all other creats

**Abilities:**
- **Legendary Form:** Transform into ultimate version (2 min duration, 1 hour cooldown)
- **Elemental Dominance:** +50% damage to opposing elements
- **Ancient Wisdom:** Sense hidden treasures and secrets
- **Legacy:** Can pass traits to offspring

**Bonding Activities:**
- **World Events:** Defend kingdoms from invasions (+100 bond)
- **Mythic Raids:** Challenge gods and titans (+150 bond)
- **Mentorship:** Train younger creats (+75 bond)
- **Retirement Planning:** Prepare for legacy system

**Prestige System (Level 99):**
- **Option 1: Rebirth** - Reset to level 1, keep 50% stats, unlock new evolution path
- **Option 2: Ascension** - Become mythic-tier, max stats, cosmetic wings/aura
- **Option 3: Legacy** - Retire creat, pass traits to new egg, unlock special breed

**Combat Activities:**
- Win race together: +15 bond

**Story Activities:**
- Complete quest together: +30 bond

- Let creat die: -30 bond
- Abandon in battle: -50 bond

- Use cursed items on creat: -75 bond

**Recovery:**
- Apologize (dialogue): +5 bond
- Give favorite food: +15 bond
- Complete redemption quest: +50 bond

## 🧪 BREEDING SYSTEM (Endgame)

### Requirements
- Two adult creats (Level 31+)
- Bond Level 60+ with both
- Breeding Stable (unlocked after clearing 4 kingdoms)
- Breeding Fee: 5,000g

### Breeding Process
1. Select two compatible creats
2. Place in breeding stable
3. Wait 48 real-world hours OR 14 in-game days
4. Receive egg with mixed traits

### Trait Inheritance
- **Element:** 50% chance of either parent's element, 5% chance of hybrid
- **Stats:** Average of both parents +10%
- **Abilities:** Random selection from both parents' movesets
- **Appearance:** Mix of both parents' features
- **Bond:** Starts at 20 (parents' reputation)

### Hybrid Elements (Rare)
- Fire + Water = Steam (5% chance)
- Fire + Air = Lightning (5% chance)
- Water + Earth = Nature (5% chance)
- Air + Earth = Sand (5% chance)
- Light + Shadow = Twilight (1% chance - mythic)

### Legendary Breeding
- Two Elder creats (Level 99) = 10% chance of Mythic offspring
- Mythic creats have unique abilities and appearances
- Can only breed once per creat lifetime

## 📊 CREAT STATS EXPLAINED

- Base: 20-80

- Affects: Physical damage dealt

**Defense:**
- Base: 10-50
- Growth: +2 per level
- Max: 250 at level 99
- Affects: Damage reduction

**Speed:**
- Base: 100-200 km/h
- Growth: +2 km/h per level
- Max: 350 km/h at level 99
- Affects: Movement speed

**Stamina:**
- Base: 50-100
- Growth: +5 per level
- Max: 500 at level 99
- Affects: Ability usage

**Element Power:**
- Base: 10-40
- Growth: +4 per level
- Max: 450 at level 99
- Affects: Elemental damage

---

### Secondary Stats

**Agility:**
- Affects: Dodge chance, turn speed
- Range: 1-100
- Calculation: (Speed + Bond) / 3

**Luck:**
- Affects: Critical hit chance, loot drops
- Range: 1-100
- Calculation: (Bond + Level) / 2

**Charisma:**
- Affects: NPC interactions, prices
- Range: 1-100
- Calculation: Bond Level

**Loyalty:**
- Affects: Obedience, flee chance
- Range: 1-100
- Calculation: Bond Level
