# Creat System - Complete Reference

**Note:** "Creat" (pronounced "creet") = short for creature

---

## 🐾 What Are Creats?

Mythical, legendary beings that bond with riders. They start as pets, grow through care and training, and eventually become mountable combat companions. Each creat has unique traits, abilities, and personality that develops based on how you raise them.

---

## 🧬 CREAT-CHARACTER SYNERGY SYSTEM

Every creat and character has unique traits that determine compatibility. Match percentage affects gameplay, attack strength, synergy unlocks, and transformation abilities.

### Match Tiers

| Match Level | Bonus | Unlocks |
|-------------|-------|---------|
| 💯 100% Match | Total Bond: All traits aligned | Max power fusion, boosted passive buffs, rare attacks unlocked |
| 🔶 75% Match | Most traits aligned | Access to combo attacks, limited duration/cooldown |
| 🔷 50% Match | Half alignment only | Can pair, but needs item boosts or temporary gear |
| ❌ <50% Match | Disallowed pairing | Cannot fuse or mount, but creat can follow as pet (no combat) |

### Match Calculation Factors

**Character Traits (100% total):**
- Elemental Affinity: 30%
- Role (Fighter, Healer, Scout, Guardian, Trickster): 20%
- Specialty (Melee, Range, Magic, Trap, Speed, Tank): 20%
- Background (Mountain, City, Sea, Sky, Underworld, Jungle, Arctic): 10%
- Trait/Skill synergy: 10%
- Armor/Gear match bonus: 10%

**Example:**
- Fire Char (melee, mountain, tank)
- Creat: Lava Liger (fire, mountain, tank, charges enemies, strong bite)
- = **100% match** 🔥💯

Unlocks: Fusion form, extra attacks, longer beast mode, full XP share, auto phantom trace sync

## 🍎 CREAT FOOD & FRUIT SYSTEM

Feeding creats specific fruits grants permanent or evolving effects like attacks, traits, and growth unlocks.

### Fruit Types

| Fruit | Function | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| 🔥 Emberfruit | Grants fire-based attack (Blaze Lash, Scorch Pulse) | Only fire-apt creats can absorb |
| 💦 Mistberry | Unlocks fog or water-based evasion | Good for stealth-type creats |
| ⚡ Zap Plum | Adds lightning-speed dashes or shock burst | Great in races or teleport mechanics |
| 🌱 Bloomseed | Boosts healing or plant-based abilities | Can poison or protect |
| 🧊 Frostfig | Adds freeze effects or slows | Best for ground control creats |
| 🌪️ Galevine | Wind-based evasion/dodge/passive speed buff | Affects race & melee |
| ☠️ Venomcore | Adds poison tail, spit, or passive damage | Mid to high level fruit |
| 🌈 Prism Peach | Rare! Unlocks dual-attack slots or multi-element traits | One per game run? 👀 |

### Synergy Boosting Foods

| Item | Effect |
|------|--------|
| 🍍 Blendberry | Temporarily boosts match % by 25% |
| 🍇 Harmony Seed | Locks synergy at 75% for one full mission |
| 🍎 Ember Apple | Shifts one trait toward fire |
| 🌈 Dreamfruit | Randomizes your creat's specialty to try new matches |
| 🧬 Echo Core | Adds a second elemental trait to your creat or char for hybrid matching |

## 🐣 CREAT GROWTH STAGES

Creats evolve through care, feeding, training, and bonding. Each stage unlocks new abilities.

### Growth Phases

| Stage | Behavior / Role | Unlocks |
|-------|----------------|---------|
| 🍼 Baby (Pet) | Follows char, small interactions | No attacks, just vibes and mini-help |
| 🍎 Learner | Can perform 1 basic help action (carry, alert, pull lever) | Needs first fruit and training |
| ⚔️ Junior Combatant | Gains 1–2 attacks, helps in story/melee | Learns from mimic training or "shadow fights" |
| 🧠 Synchronized Ally | Starts fighting beside player actively | Dodges, follows commands, sync attacks |
| 🐎 Mount-Ready | Size + loyalty high enough to ride | Opens up race, PvP, quest utility |
| 🐉 Battle Creat | Full gear unlocks, 4–6 attacks, support skills | Can power up, enter beast mode, use Phantom Trace with you |
| 🌟 Legendary Bond | 100% loyalty, special armor, name etched into bond registry | May unlock fusion or special combined move with player |

### Growth Requirements

| Requirement | Effect |
|-------------|--------|
| 🍗 Fed well (creat food/treats) | Increases loyalty |
| ⚔️ Trained (mini games, story quests) | Unlocks new moves |
| 🛡️ Protected (not KO'd too often) | Buffs resilience |
| 🎁 Equipped (helmets, gear, etc.) | Boosts stats, unlocks unique moves |
| 💖 Cared for consistently | Affects personality type (aggressive, calm, reckless, brave) |

## 🛡️ CREAT ARMOR & GEAR

Armor varies by creat type. Each creat has specific equippable slots.

| Creat Type | Gear Slots |
|------------|------------|

| 🐍 Leviathan | Helmets + fin bands |

| Gear Slot | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| Helmet | Elemental resistance + match buffer (can gain 10% match from armor) |
| Body Armor | Boosts physical match types, like melee/range balance |
| Gauntlets | Unlocks attack types, empowers Char-to-Creat command speed |
| Boots | Movement synergy, affects mount speed/agility |
| Cloak / Cape | Adds stealth, illusion, or dodge ability in match rating |

## 🎒 LOOTS NEEDED FOR OPTIMAL SYNERGY

To reach or maintain 100% bond, players must collect:

- 🎓 **Training Manuals** – specific to creat type
- 🍖 **Specialized Creat Food** – specific for traits (e.g., meat for melee/tanks, plants for healers)
- 🔮 **Bond Tokens** – dropped from boss fights, needed for fusion unlocks
- 🛡️ **Element-Sync Armor** – Char gear that enables matching and trait blending
- 📜 **Ancient Scrolls** – unlock bonus passive match traits

## 🌊 SWIMMING & TERRAIN ABILITIES

### Natural Swimmers (Default)
Can swim without needing item or gear. Can dive, leap, or dash in water zones.

| Creat Type | Swim Style | Bonus |
|------------|------------|-------|
| Leviathan (Sea Serpent) | Fast, snakelike, glide | Can dive for shortcuts |
| Amphibious Lizard/Frog Creat | Hop + kick | Can grip walls |
| Skyreign Duck-Creat or Griffin | Skims surface | Can launch from water to air |
| Water Elemental Char Race | Fluid/merged with water | Passive boost in wet zones |
| Dolphin-Shark Hybrid Creat | Rapid turn | Evasive in water-only races |

### Non-Swimmers (Default)
Require an equippable item/ability to swim or survive in water zones.

**Item Examples:**
- 🫧 Bubble Cloak (char) – lets you swim for 15–20 seconds
- 🦺 Aqua Fin Gauntlet (char or creat) – adds underwater jump/dash
- 💎 Scale Rune (creat) – adds water resistance + slippery trail behind you

**Time Limit:** 20–30 seconds in water before "drowning" animation triggers

## 💨 PHANTOM TRACE (Universal Ability)

Advanced evasion mechanic that leaves behind an illusory echo while the real player vanishes and reappears elsewhere.

### Visual Variants by Element

| Element | Trace Style | Disappear / Reappear Effect |
|---------|-------------|----------------------------|
| Fire | Burst of flame + black scorch smoke | Ash flicker + slow flame swirl |
| Air | Dust swirl + wind gust | Leaves + feather burst |
| Water | Splash fade + steam | Mist swirl on reentry |
| Earth | Pebble crumble + dust cloud | Ground crack underfoot |
| Lightning | Static zap + crackling echo | Instant blip + glowing outline |
| Shadow | Black fog + echo silhouette | Creat and char eyes glow briefly |
| Light | Flash + prism trail | Quiet hum, radiant burst |
| Poison | Green haze + toxic cloud | Dripping silhouette melts away |
| Ice | Frost puff + shimmer crack | Frozen shadow shatters |

**Upgrade Effects:**
- Fire burns for 1s
- Ice slows
- Light blinds
- Shadow fears

## 🔥 TRANSFORMATION MODE (Bloodkin Race)

A rare playable race with the ancestral ability to bond so deeply with a creat, they can temporarily become one.

### Transformation Details

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Duration | 30 seconds (base), 45s (upgraded), 60s (boss fights only) |
| Trigger | Orb charge, potion brew, ancient spell, or bond limit break |
| Effect | Dismount creat, transform into hybrid form |
| Buffs | +25% Attack Power, +15% Speed, -20% Damage Taken, Unique Skill Tree |
| Cooldown | 1-2 second fatigue stumble after revert |

### Bond Tier Progression

| Bond Tier | Transformation Benefit |
|-----------|----------------------|
| 1 (New Bond) | Base form: 30 sec limit, 1 signature move |
| 2 (Trained) | +1 hybrid skill unlocks, minor visual mutation |
| 3 (Deep Sync) | Creat fights alongside you, can combo chain |
| 4 (Ascended) | Max stat buffs, +1 passive bonus, extended time in boss zones |
| 5 (Mythic Link) | Option to permanently fuse during story climax or event 🐉🧍‍♀️🔥 |

## 🎮 CREAT BEHAVIOR IN DIFFERENT MODES

### Story Mode & Side Quests
- Creats can be summoned or fight alongside
- Help solve puzzles, reach high places, break barriers
- Gain XP and bond through shared experiences

- Mounted combat with item-based attacks
- Boost abilities for slower creats
- Terrain-specific advantages (flying creats on aerial tracks, swimmers in water zones)

### Melee / PvP
- Full combat partnership
- Transformation mode available
- Combo attacks and synergy moves

### Training Grounds
- Practice bonding
- Learn new moves
- Test gear and food effects
