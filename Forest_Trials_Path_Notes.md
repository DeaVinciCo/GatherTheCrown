# Forest Trials Path Notes (Phase 1)

## Purpose
Forest Trials should feel like one meaningful outward journey, not multiple day/night loops.

## Time Pacing
- Forest Trials now uses gradual, non-flipping time progression during each run.
- Full world day/night is not cycled repeatedly during a single trial route.
- Route target durations:
  - Gentle Trail: 8-10 minutes
  - Rider's Path: 12-15 minutes
  - Sovereign Thicket: 16-20 minutes

## Route Differences

### Gentle Trail
- Pressure profile: safer / learning-focused
- Enemy target: 4-5 foes
- Includes:
  - Wider-feel route pacing
  - Brighter tint, lighter fog, and more open tree layout
  - More starter food/support pickups
  - Wider road spacing and spread-out enemy placement
  - 1-2 crawling encounters, mixed with light walking/flying threats
  - Lower pressure combat cadence
- Reward profile: reliable supplies and confidence-building combat

### Rider's Path
- Pressure profile: balanced
- Enemy target: 5-7 foes
- Includes:
  - Mixed terrain rhythm
  - Balanced tint/fog/tree density for standard adventure feel
  - Balanced resource + combat flow
  - Standard road spacing and moderate enemy spacing
  - Mixed crawling, walking, and light flying encounters
  - Standard progression route
- Reward profile: balanced loot and crafting mats

### Sovereign Thicket
- Pressure profile: dangerous
- Enemy target: 6-8 foes
- Includes:
  - Denser threats and harsher pressure windows
  - Darker tint, denser fog, and enclosed tree layout
  - Higher tactical demand
  - Tighter road feel and more packed enemy spacing
  - Mixed walking/flying pressure with crawling threats still present
  - Stronger combat/material payoff
- Reward profile: highest risk-reward in early trials

## Fork -> Merge Flow
1. Forest Trials sign and route intent
2. Fork into 3 difficulty-marked routes
3. Route-specific gather/fight progression
4. Routes begin merging near completion
5. Strongest small-time final skirmisher is near route end and drops/unlocks Creat Egg
6. Merged trail leads to Sanctuary Isle Home Base

## Emotional Role
- Forest Trials: challenge + progression lane
- Forestry Trail: quieter support lane (gathering/survival prep)
- Both support early-game readiness before larger world responsibility opens
