# Gather The Crown: Creats & Foes - Player Compendium

Last updated: April 2026

This is the complete player-facing guide to how the game works: goals, progression, currencies, crowns, rewards, hero and creat limits, and what to do first.

## 1) What This Game Is

Gather The Crown is a fantasy action-RPG where you:
- Build a hero (Rider)
- Bond with a creat companion
- Explore outdoor and indoor zones
- Complete side quests and restoration tasks
- Defeat enemies and bosses
- Gather metal shards and elemental gems
- Forge crowns for each major game mode
- Return kingdoms to rightful rule in Story Mode

The world includes forests, roads, trails, towns, shoppes, inns, taverns, castles, ruins, and restoration locations.

## 2) Main Player Loop

The core loop is:
1. Choose route and explore
2. Fight enemies and gather loot
3. Complete side quests (find, clear, restore)
4. Return resources to NPCs/towns
5. Progress kingdom arc and castle floors
6. Defeat boss encounters
7. Collect shards and gems
8. Complete crown puzzles for major rewards
9. Repeat in higher-tier zones/modes

## 3) Story Mode Goal

Your Story Mode fantasy is to restore the realm one place at a time.

What you do in Story Mode:
- Travel outdoor routes and indoor spaces
- Help townspeople recover missing items
- Clear infested/abandoned locations
- Restore locations and hand them back to owners
- Progress to castle arcs
- Clear 2-5 castle floors per kingdom
- Resolve ruler outcome (cursed/captive/controlled/missing)
- Hand kingdom back to rightful ruler

## 4) Forest Trials and Route Difficulty

Current target run times:
- Sproutbound (Easy): 6-9 min
- Emberroot (Medium): 10-14 min
- Crownfire (Hard): 14-18 min
- Whispergrove Forage (Light trail): 10-15 min

Forage route intent:
- Mixed path terrain (woods on one side, grass/weeds on the other)
- 2-4 lower-level encounters (often 2-3 hits each)
- Gather supplies for camp/night settlement

## 5) Controls (Core)

- WASD / Arrow Keys: Move
- Space: Attack
- E: Interact (doors, exits, prompts)
- F: Feed creat
- Shift: Toggle chat / sync-related flow based on context
- I: Inventory
- M: Map
- ESC: Close/open panels or back out

## 6) Your Hero, Creats, and Character Slots

### Hero slots
- Maximum heroes per account: 3
- You begin with 1 unlocked hero slot
- Additional hero slots unlock after completing Story Mode once

### Creat rules
- Maximum creats per hero profile: 3 total
  - 1 main creat
  - 2 additional creat slots
- First creat is protected (cannot be deleted normally)
- Protected first creat can only be removed during full account deletion/reset

### Important progression gate
- First clear of Story Mode unlocks expanded account progression (multi-hero progression and post-first-run systems)

## 8) Currencies and Rewards

## Gold Coins (GC)
Main progression currency for economy actions and rewards.

How to earn GC:
- Enemy kills and combat routes
- Side quest completion
- Restoration handback rewards
- Crown completion rewards (major payouts)
- Mode participation (race, mini-games, PvE/PvP depending on content phase)

### Bixbite
High-tier currency used for advanced progression/rewards (especially crown completions and meta progression).

### Gems
Elemental collectibles used in crown puzzle requirements and progression.

### Metal shards
Crafting puzzle fragments for crowns. Different crowns require specific shard types and counts.

## 9) Major Costs and Account Rules

### Full account reset cost
- 50,000,000 GC
- Full reset is not available until Story Mode has been completed at least once

### Why this matters
- Reset is meant to be a high-stakes, intentional decision
- It also affects protected-first-creat behavior

## 10) How Crown Puzzles Work

Each crown is a requirements puzzle:
- A crown has required shard and gem types
- You collect those parts through gameplay
- Matching parts fill that crown's requirement slots
- When all required slots are filled, the crown can complete
- Completion grants large reward bundles (GC, Bixbite, items)

Key concept:
- Not all shards/gems fit every crown requirement
- Correct types and counts are required

## 17) New Player Quick Path (Recommended)

1. Forge your first hero
2. Enter Forest Trials and choose route difficulty
3. Secure your first creat progression
4. Return to Home Base and begin side quests
5. Run find/clear/restore loops for GC, loot, and materials
6. Start crown part collection intentionally (not random hoarding)
7. Complete first mode crown
8. Progress castle arc and ruler outcome
9. Finish Story Mode once to unlock full account progression (extra hero capacity)

## 18) Advanced Economy Tips

- Crown completion is one of the biggest reliable GC spikes.
- Mix route farming (steady drops) with objective rewards (burst rewards).
- Track what crown you are building before spending/selling resources.
- Save for reset only if you truly want full account restart; 50,000,000 GC is intentional end-scale cost.

## 19) Important Limits Summary

- Max heroes: 3
- Max creats per hero: 3 total (1 main + 2 additional)
- First creat: protected from deletion except full account reset path
- Account reset cost: 50,000,000 GC
- Story completion once: key unlock gate for expanded account progression

## 20) Final Summary

How to win long-term:
- Build a strong hero + creat pair
- Complete the story restoration loop kingdom by kingdom
- Learn which gems/shards complete which crowns
- Use crowns to accelerate your economy and progression
- Finish Story Mode once, then expand to multi-hero and full-depth progression systems

If you only remember one thing:
- Explore with purpose, collect with intent, forge crowns strategically, and restore the realm step by step.
