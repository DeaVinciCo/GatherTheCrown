# Awakened Human Hero Design Notes

## Canon Reset

All playable people are human.

There are no half-human hybrid races in current story canon. What changes from person to person is elemental resonance and awakening intensity.

## Core Distinction

- Awakened person: elementally resonant human.
- Hero: awakened human who chooses formal combat path, gear discipline, and certification.

Awakening is potential.
Heroism is trained choice.

## Awakening Sources

Awakening can emerge through one or several paths:

- Bloodline resonance
- Environmental exposure
- Creat-bond feedback
- Crown fragment contact
- Leyline and ruin proximity
- Emotional or traumatic threshold events
- Ritual or spiritual attunement

## Awakening Intensity Bands

- Dormant: no active manifestation
- Mild: occasional passive traits
- Active: repeatable practical ability
- Full: stable high-power expression

## Manifestation Is Not Combat-Only

Awakened citizens may never become fighters. Common uses include:

- Healing and care work
- Crafting and material shaping
- Navigation and courier service
- Investigation and sensing
- Farming and weather adaptation
- Mediation and emotional regulation

## Role Examples By Elemental Lean

1. Storm or Air awakened
- Typical paths: couriers, racers, scouts, navigators
- Traits: balance reflexes, pressure sensing, short glides, static discharge

2. Light awakened
- Typical paths: healers, teachers, diplomats, temple attendants
- Traits: emotional calming, radiant recovery, aura sensing, corruption resistance

3. Shadow awakened
- Typical paths: trackers, investigators, spies, undertakers, brokers
- Traits: stealth affinity, silence movement, low-light acuity, fear resistance

4. Earth awakened
- Typical paths: builders, smiths, guardians, farmers, protectors
- Traits: resilience, stamina depth, stone shaping, beast calming

## Hero Path Requirements

Hero candidacy requires training layers beyond awakening:

- Weapon and tool proficiency
- Element-control discipline
- Field survival and combat drills
- Creat synchronization
- Crown compatibility checks
- Kingdom certification and oath tests

## Schooling and Training Model

1. Field Study Phase (ages 11 to 12)
- Universal baseline for all youths
- Includes survival, first aid, tracking, basic tools, element identification, creat handling
- Mentors observe awakening indicators

2. Academy Phase (ages 13 to 17)
- For awakened candidates, military hopefuls, and Rider or Hero tracks
- Focus on combat doctrine, duty law, route command, and advanced resonance control

Non-Hero paths continue by apprenticeship and civic specialization:

- Healing arts
- Farming and supply work
- Smithing and craft
- Navigation and commerce
- Scholarship and records

## Four Nearby Kingdoms (Influence Model)

Kingdoms are elemental influence regions, not separate species populations.

- Aldermarch: elevated Earth and Water-adjacent awakenings
- Stormrage: elevated Storm or Air awakenings
- Sunward (Solmere alias): elevated Light and Fire awakenings
- Vastmalaise (Ashenreach frontier): elevated Shadow and Arcane-adjacent awakenings

Awakening tendency is statistical, not guaranteed.

## Sanctuary Isle Role

Sanctuary Isle is a convergence society where all awakening types can appear. It functions as neutral ground for training, recovery, relocation, retirement, and mixed-family life.

## Naming Alignment (No Runtime ID Changes)

- Keep runtime kingdom IDs and progression chains as `aldermarch`, `stormrage`, `vastmalaise`, `sunward`.
- Keep `Solmere` as cultural alias for Sunward.
- Use `Verdantwilde`, `Stormspire`, and `Ashenreach` as subregion, lineage, or citadel names unless a future full rename pass is approved.

## Implementation Migration Note

Current UI and system files may still reference legacy hybrid labels. Treat those as technical debt labels pending a text and schema migration to awakened-human terminology.
