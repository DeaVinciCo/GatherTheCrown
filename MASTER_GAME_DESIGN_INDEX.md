## 🎮 GAME OVERVIEW

### Core Pillars
1. **Racing** - High-speed creat racing with dynamic tracks
2. **Combat** - Strategic attack-based PvP and PvE
3. **Bonding** - Deep emotional connection with creats
4. **Exploration** - Discover fallen kingdoms and secrets
5. **Progression** - Multiple advancement systems
6. **Community** - Multiplayer and social features

### Unique Selling Points
- Biblical materials and faction system (authentic, meaningful)
- Creat evolution from egg to elder (emotional journey)
- 8 fallen kingdoms with cinematic backstories
- No pay-to-win (ethical monetization)
- Cross-play enabled (play with anyone)
- Deep customization (character and creat)
