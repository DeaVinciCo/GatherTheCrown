## 🎯 ETHICAL MONETIZATION PRINCIPLES

### Player-First Design
1. **No Pay-to-Win:** Skill > Money
2. **Transparent Pricing:** No hidden costs
3. **Generous Free Content:** 90% free, 10% paid
4. **Respect Time:** No artificial grinds
5. **No FOMO:** Limited items return eventually

### Fair Pricing
- Cosmetics: $5-15 (reasonable)
- Battle Pass: $5 (best value)
- Bundles: 30-50% discount
- No $100+ items (exploitative)

### Accessibility
- Free trial (unlimited)
- Regional pricing (adjust for economy)
- Frequent sales (seasonal discounts)
- Earn premium currency (play to earn)

### Community Trust
- No surprise monetization changes
- Listen to feedback
- Transparent roadmap
- Regular free updates
