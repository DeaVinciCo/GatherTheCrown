## 🏁 RACE MODES

### 1. Quick Race (Casual)
**Players:** 4-8  
**Duration:** 5-10 minutes  
**Ranked:** No

**Rules:**
- Random track selection
- 3 laps standard
- Combat allowed
- No entry fee
- Casual matchmaking

**Rewards:**
- 100-300g based on placement
- 500-1,000 XP
- Random item drops (common-rare)

**Matchmaking:**
- Skill-based (hidden MMR)
- Region priority
- Quick queue (30 seconds max wait)

### 3. Team Race (2v2 or 4v4)
**Players:** 4 or 8  
**Duration:** 10-15 minutes  
**Ranked:** Optional

**Rules:**

- Combined score determines winner

**Scoring:**
- 1st place: 10 points
- 2nd place: 8 points

### 5. Elimination Race
**Players:** 8-16  
**Duration:** 15-20 minutes  
**Ranked:** No (too chaotic)

**Rules:**
- Every 60 seconds, last place is eliminated
- Combat heavily encouraged
- Continuous laps until 1 player remains
- High risk, high reward

**Elimination Timer:**
```
LAP 1: All 16 players
60s: 15 players (1 eliminated)
120s: 14 players
180s: 13 players
...
FINAL: 1 winner remains
```

**Rewards:**
- Winner: 3,000g, 10,000 XP, guaranteed epic item
- Top 3: 1,500g, 5,000 XP
- Top 8: 500g, 2,000 XP
- Eliminated: 100g, 500 XP

**Matchmaking:**
- Open to all skill levels
- First-come, first-served
- Chaos mode (anything goes)

## ⚔️ COMBAT MODES

**Duration:** 5-10 minutes  
**Ranked:** Yes

**Rules:**

- Winner: 500g, 2,000 XP, +25 ELO
- Loser: 200g, 800 XP, -15 ELO

**Matchmaking:**
- ELO-based
- Best of 3 format
- Rematch option

**Matchmaking:**
- Team average ELO

## 🏆 BATTLE ROYALE MODE

### Crown Royale (Battle Royale)
**Players:** 32  
**Duration:** 20-30 minutes  
**Ranked:** Seasonal leaderboard

**Rules:**
- Last rider standing wins
- Shrinking elemental zone
- Loot scattered across map
- Solo, Duo, or Squad (4-player teams)

**Map:**
- Large open world (5km²)
- All 8 kingdom zones represented
- Dynamic weather and hazards
- Loot tiers: Common → Mythic

**Phases:**
```
PHASE 1 (0-5 min): Safe zone full map
- Loot and prepare
- 32 players alive

PHASE 2 (5-10 min): Zone shrinks 50%
- Combat begins
- ~20 players alive

PHASE 3 (10-15 min): Zone shrinks 75%
- Intense fighting
- ~10 players alive

PHASE 4 (15-20 min): Zone shrinks 90%
- Final circle
- ~5 players alive

PHASE 5 (20+ min): Tiny final zone
- Last stand
- Winner determined
```

**Loot System:**
- Weapons: Common → Legendary
- Armor: Tiers 1-5
- Attacks: Random drops
- Healing: Potions and food
- Creat gear: Saddles and armor

**Rewards:**
- Winner: 5,000g, 15,000 XP, legendary item, "Royale Champion" title
- Top 5: 2,500g, 8,000 XP, epic item
- Top 10: 1,000g, 4,000 XP, rare item
- Participation: 300g, 1,000 XP

**Matchmaking:**
- Open to all
- Skill-based lobbies (hidden MMR)
- Cross-play enabled
- Long queue times (need 32 players)

## 🎯 MATCHMAKING SYSTEM

### Skill-Based Matchmaking (SBMM)

**Hidden MMR (Matchmaking Rating):**
- Starts at 1000
- Increases with wins, decreases with losses
- Factors: Win rate, placement, performance
- Separate MMR for each mode

**Matchmaking Priorities:**
1. Skill (MMR ±100)
2. Region (lower ping)
3. Wait time (expand search after 2 minutes)
4. Party size (match groups with groups)

---

### Ranked System

**Seasons:**
- Duration: 3 months
- Soft reset: Drop 1 tier, keep progress
- Placement matches: 10 games to determine starting rank
- Season rewards: Exclusive items, titles, currency

**Ranked Points:**
- Win: +25 to +50 (based on opponent MMR)
- Loss: -15 to -30
- Streak bonus: +5 per win (max +25)
- Demotion protection: 3 losses at 0 points

**Leaderboards:**
- Top 100: Public leaderboard
- Top 10: Special recognition
- #1: "Crown Champion" title, exclusive rewards

---

### Party System

**Party Sizes:**
- Solo: Queue alone
- Duo: 2 players
- Squad: 3-4 players
- Raid: 5-8 players

**Party Features:**
- Voice chat (optional)
- Text chat
- Shared quest progress

- Leader can invite/kick

**Party Bonuses:**
- +10% XP for duo
- +15% XP for squad
