# Sanctuary Isle Governance Charter

Status: Canon Locked  
Date: 2026-05-14  
Scope: Sanctuary Isle political structure, succession, civic process, and annual civic rites

---

## 1. Core Principle: Sovereign Sanctity

Sanctuary Isle is self-governed.

Sovereign Sanctity means the isle keeps authority over its own civic order, shelter law, and community protections, while still negotiating with outside kingdoms through a designated envoy role.

Foundational civic promise:
- No resident is denied protection, schooling, or due process because of lineage, awakening profile, district origin, or former kingdom affiliation.

---

## 2. Decision Model (Locked)

Sanctuary uses Towne Hall governance with a four-seat Major Council.

Major decisions require the participation of all four seats:
- Talli (Administrative Legitimacy Seat)
- Juniper (Social Cohesion Seat)
- Calder (Logistics and Security Seat)
- Harbor Speaker (External Voice Seat)

If all four are present, a major decision passes with 3 of 4 votes.

If fewer than four seats are present, only temporary emergency measures are allowed and must be re-voted at the next Towne Hall.

Examples of major decisions:
- Inter-kingdom treaty acceptance or refusal
- Dock lockdown, route closure, or militia mobilization
- School charter revision
- New district tax, rationing, or emergency levy
- Permanent changes to shelter and belonging law

Routine district operations are handled by local stewards and do not require a major vote.

---

## 3. Official Leadership Title and Succession Method

### Title
Harbor Speaker of Sanctuary Isle

### Role
- Speaks for Sanctuary in external political matters.
- Carries Towne Hall resolutions to other kingdoms and trade powers.
- Cannot unilaterally overrule internal law.

### Succession Method
The Harbor Speaker is chosen by Towne Hall confirmation in three steps:

1. Nomination Window
- Any recognized district steward may nominate one candidate.

2. Public Question Session
- Open Towne Hall where residents question candidates on neutrality, route safety, and sanctuary law.

3. Confirmation Vote
- The three standing seats (Talli, Juniper, Calder) confirm by at least 2 of 3 votes.
- If confirmation fails, an interim Harbor Speaker serves up to 90 days and a new nomination window opens.

Emergency vacancy rule:
- If Harbor Speaker is absent, Juniper acts as temporary public voice for civic continuity, while Calder handles route and dock advisories.

## 4. Inaugural Council Roster (Canon)

Major Council seats at charter ratification:
- Talli: Administrative Legitimacy Seat
- Juniper: Social Cohesion Seat
- Calder: Logistics and Security Seat
- Harbor Jae: Harbor Speaker of Sanctuary Isle

Advisory regulars (non-voting unless formally seated during vacancy procedures):
- Old Ren (Elders and Tide Memory)
- Linna (Care and Apothecary)
- Rowan (Training and Readiness)
- Mirel (Hatchling and Family Welfare)

---

## 5. Towne Hall Meeting Cadence

Standard cadence:
- Monthly Towne Hall for policy review, route reports, and district petitions.

Seasonal cadence:
- One Grand Towne Hall at each season-turn for annual budget, school placement pressure, and route risk forecasts.

Emergency cadence:
- A bell-call session may be convened by any two Major Council seats.

Public transparency rule:
- Every major vote must be recorded in the Towne Ledger and posted to market board copy by next morning shift.

---

## 6. Three Yearly Island Events Tied to Governance

### Event 1: Lantern Covenant Night (Early Spring)
Purpose:
- Renewal of Sovereign Sanctity vows and public reading of shelter law.

Gameplay hooks:
- Community escort routes between Little Lantern House, market lane, and Towne Hall.
- Dialogue checks around belonging disputes resolved by civic oath.

Governance tie:
- Major Council presents annual civic commitments and receives petition scrolls.

### Event 2: Tide Accord Assembly (Midsummer)
Purpose:
- Route, dock, and trade policy summit before heavy travel season.

Gameplay hooks:
- Dock logistics mini-events, cargo prioritization choices, and weather risk briefings.

Governance tie:
- Harbor Speaker delivers external dispatches and treaty updates for vote.

### Event 3: Ember Mercy Trials (Late Autumn)
Purpose:
- Public readiness demonstrations paired with restorative justice hearings.

Gameplay hooks:
- Open Ring and Trial Circle challenge boards tied to non-lethal discipline standards.

Governance tie:
- Calder and Juniper co-chair emergency readiness and community repair resolutions.

## 8. Canon Summary (Short Form)

- Sanctuary Isle is self-governed under Sovereign Sanctity.
- Towne Hall is the decision arena.
- Four seats decide major policy: Talli, Juniper, Calder, Harbor Speaker.
- Inaugural Harbor Speaker is Harbor Jae.
- Major votes require all four present and pass on 3 of 4.
- Civic legitimacy is maintained through posted ledger transparency.
