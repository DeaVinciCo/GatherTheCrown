# 🌳 Skill Tree System - Gather the Crown: Creats & Foes

**System:** Deep character progression through skill trees  
**Purpose:** Customize playstyle and unlock powerful abilities  
**Created:** February 25, 2026

---

## 🎯 SKILL TREE OVERVIEW

### Core Concept
- **Skill Points:** Earned through leveling up (1 point per level)
- **Total Points:** 99 points (level 1-99)
- **Respec Cost:** 1,000 gold (can reset once per week)
- **Skill Tiers:** 5 tiers, unlock as you progress
- **Prerequisites:** Some skills require others first

---

## 🌲 SKILL TREE STRUCTURE

### Tree Categories

**1. Combat Tree** ⚔️
- Focus: Damage, critical hits, weapon mastery
- For: Aggressive players, DPS builds

**2. Defense Tree** 🛡️
- Focus: HP, armor, damage reduction
- For: Tank players, survival builds

**3. Speed Tree** 💨
- Focus: Movement speed, acceleration, agility
- For: Racing specialists, evasion builds

**4. Elemental Tree** 🔥
- Focus: Elemental damage, resistances, special effects
- For: Magic users, elemental builds

**5. Utility Tree** 🔧
- Focus: Gold gain, XP boost, crafting bonuses
- For: Economy players, completionists

**6. Creat Bond Tree** 🐾
- Focus: Creat stats, bond bonuses, evolution
- For: Creat specialists, breeders
