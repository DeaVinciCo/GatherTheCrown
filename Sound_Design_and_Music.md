### Kingdom Themes (8 Unique Tracks)

**1. Sylvara (Forest Kingdom)**
- **Style:** Celtic folk + orchestral
- **Instruments:** Flute, harp, strings, nature sounds
- **Mood:** Mystical, peaceful, corrupted (minor key shift)
- **Tempo:** Moderate (100 BPM)
- **Variations:** Peaceful (exploration), Corrupted (combat)

**2. Pyrrathia (Lava Kingdom)**
- **Style:** Heavy metal + orchestral
- **Instruments:** Electric guitar, drums, brass, anvil hits
- **Mood:** Aggressive, intense, powerful
- **Tempo:** Fast (140 BPM)
- **Variations:** Forge (ambient), Battle (intense)

**3. Frostvale (Ice Kingdom)**
- **Style:** Nordic folk + ambient
- **Instruments:** Hardanger fiddle, choir, bells, wind sounds
- **Mood:** Cold, beautiful, melancholic
- **Tempo:** Slow (80 BPM)
- **Variations:** Frozen (ambient), Thaw (hopeful)

**4. Zerath Dunes (Desert Kingdom)**
- **Style:** Middle Eastern + orchestral
- **Instruments:** Oud, darbuka, ney flute, strings
- **Mood:** Exotic, mysterious, desperate
- **Tempo:** Moderate (110 BPM)
- **Variations:** Oasis (peaceful), Sandstorm (chaotic)

**5. Mor'gahl Fen (Swamp Kingdom)**
- **Style:** Bayou blues + dark ambient
- **Instruments:** Harmonica, banjo, didgeridoo, synth pads
- **Mood:** Eerie, toxic, tragic
- **Tempo:** Slow (90 BPM)
- **Variations:** Murk (ambient), Poison (tense)

**6. Luminaris (Crystal Kingdom)**
- **Style:** Electronic + orchestral (ethereal)
- **Instruments:** Synthesizers, glass harmonica, choir, strings
- **Mood:** Radiant, magical, shattered
- **Tempo:** Moderate (105 BPM)
- **Variations:** Prism (beautiful), Shattered (broken)

**7. Umbral Reach (Shadow Kingdom)**
- **Style:** Gothic + dark ambient
- **Instruments:** Organ, choir, cello, synth bass
- **Mood:** Dark, mysterious, misunderstood
- **Tempo:** Slow (85 BPM)
- **Variations:** Twilight (calm), Void (terrifying)

**8. Crown Convergence (Final Kingdom)**
- **Style:** All styles fused + epic orchestral
- **Instruments:** Full orchestra + all cultural instruments
- **Mood:** Epic, unified, climactic
- **Tempo:** Variable (builds from 90 to 150 BPM)
- **Variations:** Ruins (sad), Restored (triumphant)

### Dynamic Music System

**Adaptive Layers:**
- Base layer: Ambient (exploration)
- Combat layer: Percussion + intensity
- Boss layer: Full orchestra + choir
- Victory layer: Triumphant resolution

**Transitions:**
- Smooth crossfades (2-4 seconds)
- Musical bridges between sections
- Tempo matching for seamless flow

**Context-Aware:**
- Low HP: Music becomes tense
- Boost active: Music intensifies
- Final lap: Music builds to climax
- Victory: Triumphant fanfare

## 🔊 SOUND EFFECTS

### Creat Sounds (Per Species)

**Fire Creats:**
- Movement: Crackling flames, ember sparks
- Attack: Roar + fire whoosh
- Damage taken: Pained growl + sizzle
- Death: Fading embers, sad whimper
- Idle: Breathing fire, content rumble

**Water Creats:**
- Movement: Splashing, water flow
- Attack: Aquatic roar + wave crash
- Damage taken: Gurgle + splash
- Death: Water dispersing, final bubble
- Idle: Gentle waves, breathing

**Air Creats:**
- Movement: Wind rush, feather flutter
- Attack: Screech + sonic boom
- Damage taken: Wind knocked out
- Death: Fading whistle, falling
- Idle: Gentle breeze, wing flaps

**Earth Creats:**
- Movement: Heavy footsteps, rock grinding
- Attack: Rumble + earthquake
- Damage taken: Rock cracking
- Death: Crumbling stone, thud
- Idle: Deep breathing, shifting weight

---

### Combat Sounds

**Attacks:**
- Flame Lash: Fire whip crack + whoosh
- Ice Spike: Crystalline shard + impact
- Lightning Bolt: Electric zap + thunder
- Rock Spike: Earth rumble + stone break
- Wind Surge: Gust + speed boost
- Poison Cloud: Hiss + toxic bubble
- Light Beam: Radiant hum + flash
- Shadow Strike: Void whisper + dark impact

**Impacts:**
- Hit enemy: Thud + damage indicator
- Critical hit: Enhanced impact + chime
- Miss: Whoosh + disappointment sound
- Block: Shield clang + deflect
- Dodge: Quick swoosh + relief

**Status Effects:**
- Burn: Crackling fire loop
- Freeze: Ice cracking, shivering
- Poison: Bubbling toxin, coughing
- Stun: Electric buzz, dazed
- Slow: Heavy movement, sluggish
- Heal: Gentle chime, restoration

---

### Racing Sounds

**Movement:**
- Acceleration: Engine rev (creat-appropriate)
- Deceleration: Wind down, braking
- Drift: Tire screech (or claw scrape)
- Jump: Whoosh up, land with thud
- Crash: Impact, debris, damage

**Boost:**
- Activation: Power surge, energy burst
- Active: High-speed wind, engine roar
- Depletion: Power down, return to normal

**Terrain:**
- Asphalt: Standard hoofbeats/claws
- Grass: Softer, rustling
- Sand: Muffled, shifting
- Ice: Slippery, sliding
- Lava: Sizzling, bubbling
- Water: Splashing, swimming

---

### UI Sounds

**Menu Navigation:**
- Hover: Soft chime
- Select: Confirmation beep

**HUD Feedback:**
