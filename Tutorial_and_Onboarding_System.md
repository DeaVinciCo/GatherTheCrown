### Phase 3: Interactive Tutorial (30-45 minutes)

## 📖 TUTORIAL STRUCTURE

### Tutorial 1: The Awakening (5 minutes)
**Location:** Your village (safe zone)

**Objectives:**
1. Move around (WASD or stick)
2. Look around (mouse or right stick)
3. Interact with Elder Miriam (E or button)
4. Pick up the egg (tutorial item)

**Teaching Methods:**
- On-screen prompts (contextual)
- Highlighted objects (glow effect)
- NPC guidance (Elder Miriam)
- No combat yet (safe exploration)

**Dialogue:**
- Elder: "Welcome, young rider. The legends speak of you."
- Elder: "This egg... it's been waiting for someone special."
- Elder: "Take it. Your destiny begins now."

**Completion Reward:**
- Mysterious Egg (quest item)
- 100 XP
- "First Steps" achievement

---

### Tutorial 2: The Hatching (5 minutes)
**Location:** Village shrine

**Objectives:**
1. Place egg on altar
2. Perform hatching ritual (button sequence)
3. Name your creat
4. Watch hatching cutscene (30 seconds)

**Teaching Methods:**
- Button prompts (QTE-style)
- Emotional moment (music swells)
- Cutscene (skippable after first view)
- Bond meter introduced

**Dialogue:**
- Elder: "Speak its name, and the bond will form."
- [Player names creat]
- Elder: "A perfect match. You two are destined for greatness."

**Completion Reward:**
- Your first creat (hatchling)
- Bond level 10
- "First Bond" achievement
- 200 XP

---

### Tutorial 3: Basic Movement (5 minutes)
**Location:** Village training grounds

**Objectives:**
1. Walk with your creat (follows you)
2. Run (sprint button)
3. Jump (jump button)
4. Interact with creat (pet, feed)

**Teaching Methods:**
- Obstacle course (simple)
- Creat reacts to actions
- Positive reinforcement (creat happy sounds)
- No time pressure

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- Movement speed
- Stamina bar (sprinting)
- Creat following AI
- Bonding actions

**Dialogue:**
- Trainer: "Your creat will follow you everywhere."
- Trainer: "Try petting it. Bonding is important."
- [Creat makes happy sound]
- Trainer: "See? It likes you already!"

**Completion Reward:**
- Basic food (5x)
- 300 XP
- Unlock: Creat care menu

---

### Tutorial 4: First Combat (10 minutes)
**Location:** Village outskirts

**Objectives:**
1. Equip weapon (starter sword)
2. Attack training dummy (light/heavy attacks)
3. Block incoming attack (shield)
4. Dodge roll (evade)
5. Use first attack ability (Flame Lash)

**Teaching Methods:**
- Training dummies (can't die)
- Slow-motion on first successful actions
- Visual feedback (damage numbers)
- Instructor guidance

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- Light attack (fast, low damage)
- Heavy attack (slow, high damage)
- Block (reduce damage)
- Dodge (invincibility frames)
- Attack abilities (cooldowns)

**Dialogue:**
- Instructor: "Every rider must know how to fight."
- Instructor: "Light attacks are fast. Heavy attacks hit harder."
- Instructor: "Timing your dodge is key to survival."

**Completion Reward:**
- Starter sword (equipped)
- Flame Lash attack (equipped)
- 500 XP
- Unlock: Combat menu

---

### Tutorial 5: First Race (10 minutes)
**Location:** Village race track

**Objectives:**
1. Mount your creat (now rideable)
2. Accelerate and brake
3. Turn and drift
4. Use boost
5. Complete 1 lap

**Teaching Methods:**
- Simple oval track
- No opponents (solo practice)
- Boost pads highlighted
- Checkpoints guide you

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- Mounting/dismounting
- Acceleration/braking
- Steering
- Drifting basics
- Boost meter

**Dialogue:**
- Race Master: "Your creat is strong enough to ride now!"
- Race Master: "Feel the wind in your hair. This is freedom!"
- [Creat makes excited sound]

**Completion Reward:**
- Basic saddle (equipped)
- 750 XP
- Unlock: Racing menu
- "First Ride" achievement

---

### Tutorial 6: The Attack (10 minutes)
**Location:** Village (under attack)

**Objectives:**
1. Defend villagers from corrupted creats
2. Use combat and racing together
3. Defeat 5 corrupted wolves
4. Escape the village (race to exit)

**Teaching Methods:**
- Real combat (can die, but respawn)
- Combines all learned skills
- Urgency (village burning)
- Emotional stakes (save people)

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- Mounted combat
- Enemy AI
- Health management
- Death and respawn

**Dialogue:**
- Elder: "They're here! Protect the villagers!"
- Kael: "I'll help you! Let's fight together!"
- Elder: "Go! Save yourself! The world needs you!"

**Completion Reward:**
- 1,000 XP
- Reach level 3
- Unlock: World map
- "Survivor" achievement

## 💡 TEACHING METHODS

### Contextual Tooltips
- Appear when relevant
- Dismissible (X button)
- Can be reviewed later (help menu)
- Fade after 10 seconds

**Example:**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ 💡 TIP: Drifting                │
│                                 │
│ Hold brake + turn to drift.    │
│ Longer drifts = more boost!    │
│                                 │
│ [Got it!]  [Show me]  [Dismiss] │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### Visual Indicators
- Glowing objects (interact here)
- Arrows (go this way)
- Circles (stand here)
- Outlines (important NPCs)

**Color Coding:**
- Yellow: Objective
- Blue: Optional
- Green: Friendly
- Red: Enemy
- Purple: Rare/Important

## 🚀 ONBOARDING BEST PRACTICES

### Pacing
- Introduce 1-2 mechanics at a time
- Allow practice before moving on
- No information overload
- Breaks between tutorials (exploration)

### Feedback
- Positive reinforcement (praise)
- Clear success indicators
- Gentle failure messages
- Encourage experimentation

### Accessibility
- Adjustable difficulty (tutorial)
- Skip options (after first attempt)
- Colorblind-friendly indicators
- Text-to-speech (optional)

### Engagement
- Story-driven (not dry)
- Emotional moments (hatching)
- Immediate rewards
- Sense of progression
