# 🎮 Gather the Crown: Controls Guide

## How to Play

Open your browser and go to: **http://localhost:3001**

## Game Modes

### 🏁 Race Mode
Fast-paced racing with your Creat through challenging tracks!

**Controls:**
- **W / Arrow Up** - Accelerate forward
- **S / Arrow Down** - Brake / Reverse
- **A / Arrow Left** - Turn left
- **D / Arrow Right** - Turn right
- **Space** - Jump
- **Shift** - Boost (1.8x speed)

**Features:**
- Smooth third-person camera that follows your Creat
- Jump over obstacles
- Speed boost for intense racing
- See your hero riding the Creat!

---

### ⚔️ Combat Mode
Battle enemy Creats in the arena!

**Controls:**
- **WASD / Arrow Keys** - Move in all directions
- **Space** - Attack (1 second cooldown)
- **Shift** - Dodge backward (2 second cooldown)

**Features:**
- Move freely around the arena
- Enemy AI that chases and attacks you
- Dodge enemy attacks
- Health bars for both you and the enemy
- Victory/defeat conditions

---

### 🐉 Creat Bonding Mode
Bond with your Creat and strengthen your relationship!

**Controls:**
- **Space** - Interact/Bond

**Features:**
- Increase bond level with your Creat
- Watch your Creat respond to your care
- Build trust and loyalty

---

### 🏛️ Vault Exploration Mode
Explore ancient vaults and discover treasures!

**Controls:**
- **WASD / Arrow Keys** - Move around
- **Space** - Interact with objects

**Features:**
- Discover hidden treasures
- Explore mysterious vaults
- Uncover kingdom secrets

---

## Tips

- In Race Mode, use Shift for boost on straightaways
- In Combat Mode, keep moving to avoid enemy attacks
- Use dodge (Shift) when the enemy gets close
- Jump to avoid obstacles or just for fun!
- Watch your health bars in combat
